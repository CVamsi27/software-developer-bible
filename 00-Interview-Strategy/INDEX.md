## Which interview-prep lane should I use?

This section (00-Interview-Strategy) is **cross-lane** — it applies to every interview. After you finish here, pick the lane that matches your target job market:

| Lane | Folder | When to use |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Indian SDE / FAANG** | [31-SDE-Role/](../31-SDE-Role/) | Targeting Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta, Apple, or in-country India roles. LeetCode-heavy, 8-week intensive, USD 150K-400K+. |
| **Abroad Full-Stack** | [32-Abroad-Full-Stack-Lane/](../32-Abroad-Full-Stack-Lane/) | Targeting EU / non-Indian mid-stage startups (n8n, SumUp, Scalable, sennder, etc.). Take-home + system design, 4-week plan, EUR 70-110K + visa sponsorship. |

# Resume Tips for Senior Full Stack Engineers

A screener spends seconds deciding whether your resume matches the role. Compare "Responsible for API performance" with "Removed repeated catalog queries with a bounded cache; p95 fell from 800 ms to 120 ms" — the second names a mechanism and evidence a reader can verify in interview.

```text
job requirement → relevant project → your contribution → supported outcome
```

## Definition

A **resume** is a short screening document that connects **evidence** of your work to the target **role**. It should help a reader find your responsibilities, contribution and results, and support the later interview discussion.

> **Textbook Definition:** A resume is a selective professional record that summarizes a candidate's qualifications, employment and contributions for evaluation against a role's requirements.

## First Principles

The reader has limited attention, so put the most relevant evidence where it is easy to find. Claims must survive follow-up: use truthful scope and observable outcomes rather than invented metrics. Each role values different evidence, so tailor emphasis from a complete record while keeping the facts consistent.

## How It Works

### 1. Build From Evidence

Start with a role's requirements, then select the projects that demonstrate them. For each project, record the problem, your action, the result and how you know the result. This evidence also becomes material for [STAR stories](00.03-star-method.md).

Those numbers above are an example, not values to copy. When exact measurements are unavailable, use a concrete result such as adoption by four teams, removal of a manual step or an incident's verified recovery. Do not turn an estimate into a precise percentage.

### 2. Arrange the First Page

Use clear contact information, a short role-specific summary where useful, relevant experience, grouped skills and education. Add projects, publications or certifications when they supply evidence the experience section does not.

A summary should state work you can substantiate: domain, scale and a significant contribution. Within a role, give enough product context to understand the achievement, then keep bullets focused on changes you helped produce. Group skills by language, frontend, backend, data and platform when that matches the target.

### 3. Make Senior Scope Visible

Name the system or service you owned, its scale or risk, the decision you made, the teams you aligned and the operational result. Show mentoring through an outcome, such as an engineer taking independent ownership or a review practice catching a class of defect; a seniority adjective alone is weak evidence. A relevant older technology is legitimate experience; avoid claiming expertise from a brief workshop.

### 4. Make the File Readable and Parseable

Application systems differ in parsing and screening behavior; there is no universal rejection percentage or fixed recruiter scan time. Use standard headings, consistent dates and simple layouts. Follow the employer's requested file format, and if submitting PDF, verify both its rendering and extracted text order.

| Check | Why |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Plain, readable section headings | Humans and parsers can identify the content |
| Accurate role vocabulary | Relevant experience is discoverable without keyword stuffing |
| Consistent dates, titles and links | The record can be compared across documents |
| Readable font and spacing | Density does not make the evidence hard to scan |
| Correct PDF/text extraction | Visual correctness alone does not prove parsing order |

Use one or two pages according to relevant evidence and context; a ten-year threshold is not a rule. Remove repetition before shrinking the font.

### 5. Tailor and Validate

Maintain one comprehensive achievement record, then choose material for each role. If the job asks for observability, describe genuine monitoring or tracing work in recognizable language — do not add tools you cannot discuss. Ask a peer to scan the resume briefly and explain what role, scale and contribution they remember. Check that every prominent claim has a supporting story, and verify contact details, links, spelling, dates and the exported file before submitting.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Repair |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Listing responsibilities only | Explain a change and its outcome |
| Inventing numbers to satisfy a metric quota | Use verifiable qualitative or quantitative evidence |
| Listing every tool ever touched | Select relevant tools and truthful depth |
| Copying job keywords without experience | Show where the skill was actually applied |
| Packing in unrelated history | Keep evidence that helps assess this role |
| Sending an unchecked export | Inspect rendering, text order and links |

## Summary

Build the resume from evidence, place relevant contributions early, and use a readable format that survives export and parsing. Make senior scope explicit through owned systems, decisions and operational outcomes rather than adjectives. Tailor emphasis without changing facts, then test whether a reader can explain your value and whether you can defend each claim.

# Find Startup and Product Roles Without a Stale Job List

A promising opening can be real on Monday and closed before your application is read. The failure is rarely a shortage of job boards; it is treating a copied posting as the employer's current state. A useful search therefore starts at the source that owns the vacancy, then records what it says today.

```text
employer ATS (authority)
  → startup directory or aggregator (discovery)
  → community or referral (context)
  → dated role record (decision)
```

## Definition

A **job channel** is a discovery path that leads to a dated, identifiable opening. A **posting** is an employer's or intermediary's representation of that opening at one point in time. The employer's applicant tracking system (ATS) owns the vacancy state; directories and aggregators mostly copy it.

> **Textbook Definition:** A job search channel is a source or method that exposes employment opportunities; its usefulness depends on coverage, freshness, and the authority of the source that owns the vacancy.

## First Principles

Only the employer can close, reopen, or change a role. Every other channel is a cache. Caches are useful for discovery because they aggregate many employers, but they age independently and can retain a filled role or miss a new one. A senior search optimizes for **verified current openings that fit hard constraints**, not for the largest possible list.

## 1. Choose the Channel by Authority, Not Volume

| Channel | Best for | Authority | Typical failure |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Employer careers page and ATS | Confirming the role, team, location and application state | Owns the vacancy | Poor discovery if you do not know the company |
| Startup directory | Finding companies by stage, market, location or batch | Copies employer data | Stale profiles and closed roles |
| General aggregator | Broad keyword and location coverage | Copies or scrapes employer data | Duplicates, expired posts, recruiter reposts |
| Community and referral | Context, hidden roles and a named contact | Human, but unverified | Out-of-date gossip and invented urgency |
| Recruiter or agency | Roles not publicly listed | Contract-dependent | Role identity and exclusivity are unclear |

Start with directories to discover **companies**, then resolve each interesting role on the employer's own careers page. This two-step order keeps discovery broad without trusting the copy that produced it.

### Startup-focused sources

These are stable **channels**, not a list of current vacancies. Open them, check the date and location filters, and follow each result back to the employer.

- [Work at a Startup](https://www.workatastartup.com/) — Y Combinator's startup job platform; company stage, role and compensation fields vary by post.
- [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/jobs) — startup-focused roles with company, equity and remote filters; verify the employer's own posting.
- [Welcome to the Jungle](https://www.welcometothejungle.com/en) — European product and tech roles with company profiles.
- [startup.jobs](https://startup.jobs/) — global startup listings; useful for discovery, not as final evidence.
- [Y Combinator company directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies) — find companies by batch, industry and location, then check their careers page.
- [Hacker News "Who is hiring?"](https://hnhiring.com/) — monthly community threads; the poster may be an engineer rather than a recruiter.
- [Berlin Startup Jobs](https://berlinstartupjobs.com/) and [GermanTechJobs](https://www.germantechjobs.de/) — German market discovery; confirm the role and work authorization with the employer.
- [HasJob](https://hasjob.co/), [Cutshort](https://cutshort.io/) and [Instahyre](https://instahyre.com/) — Indian startup and product-company discovery.
- [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/), [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/) and [Welcome to the Jungle](https://www.welcometothejungle.com/en) — broad reach; expect reposts and stale copies.

A board's presence proves only that someone published a listing. It does not prove the employer is actively interviewing, that the team still owns the product, or that the salary band is current.

## 2. Resolve the Posting to Its Owner

An ATS URL usually contains the employer's board name and a role identifier. Common shapes are `boards.greenhouse.io/<company>`, `jobs.ashbyhq.com/<company>`, `jobs.lever.co/<company>` and `<company>.jobs.personio.com`. The exact path is less important than the check: the same role should exist on the employer's domain or official board, with a stable identifier.

Record these fields before tailoring anything:

| Field | Evidence to capture |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Employer and entity | Careers page, legal entity, role location |
| Role identity | Exact title, requisition or job ID, team, seniority |
| Source and date | ATS URL plus the date you opened it |
| State | Open, closed, reposted, uncertain, or historical |
| Hard constraints | Work authorization, location, on-call, travel, contract type |
| Compensation | Published range or written recruiter statement, with currency |
| Next action | One question or application step with an owner and date |

When the ATS and the aggregator disagree, the ATS wins for existence and the employer wins for conditions. Ask the recruiter about anything material that the posting omits rather than filling the gap with a plausible assumption.

## 3. Read a Startup Role for Ownership and Runway

"Startup" covers a pre-seed team and a post-Series C company with hundreds of employees. The label does not tell you what the role owns, how decisions are made, or whether the company can fund the role through its expected hiring cycle. Ask dated, role-specific questions:

- What product or service does this team own, and who uses it?
- Why is the role open now: growth, replacement, reorganisation, or a new bet?
- Which decisions can this role make without another approval?
- Who is on call, and what is the expected response boundary?
- What is the current funding position, and which source or date supports it?
- How are salary, bonus and equity determined for this level?
- Does this specific opening support the work authorization it requires?

Title inflation is common. A "founding engineer" may own infrastructure, product delivery and support, while a "senior engineer" at a larger company may own one service. Neither is automatically better; compare the actual responsibilities, support and compensation with your target.

Equity needs its own model. Ask for the grant size, strike price, vesting schedule, cliff, preferred-share valuation and liquidation terms where available. A percentage without a valuation and preference stack is not a cash-equivalent number, and a funding announcement is not proof of runway.

## 4. Verify Before You Invest Time

Treat an unsolicited listing as a claim to check, especially when it arrives by direct message:

- The role appears on the employer's official ATS or careers page.
- The recruiter's email domain matches the employer, or the agency's relationship is explicit.
- No one asks you to pay to apply, buy equipment, deposit a cheque, or move cryptocurrency.
- The interview process is described consistently by the recruiter and the hiring manager.
- The role has a real team, product and legal entity behind it.

A repost is not automatically fraud; agencies and aggregators repost legitimate roles. It is a reason to check the owner and the date, not a reason to skip the role. Conversely, a polished careers page does not prove the vacancy is active.

## 5. Run a Small, Measurable Pipeline

Track roles rather than application volume. For each record keep the source, date, state, fit evidence, unresolved constraint and next action. Review the pipeline weekly: close dead roles, follow up on promised dates, and replace low-fit volume with a targeted referral or a better source.

A referral works when the ask is specific: name the role, state one relevant piece of evidence, and ask whether the person can refer you or point you to the owner. Do not ask a stranger to "review your resume" without a role.

For repeat monitoring, the repository's [job-board watch](../docs/scripts-map.md) queries public Greenhouse, Ashby and Personio boards and distinguishes a reopened title from a role that disappeared. Keep the watch list in the private workspace: applied-to roles and recruiter names are personal data, not repository content.

## 6. Use Tooling Without Outsourcing the Decision

A local job-search workspace keeps discovery, evaluation and application state in files you control. [career-ops](https://github.com/career-ops-hq/career-ops) is a public MIT-licensed example: it scans public ATS boards such as Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever, checks whether a posting is still live, scores a role against your CV, and drafts a tailored CV or cover letter. Its documented boundary is draft-only: the candidate reviews and submits. Similar repos differ in sources, models and storage; compare their guarantees rather than their feature lists.

The repeatable work is worth automating:

- **Discovery:** query several public boards, deduplicate reposts and keep the source URL.
- **Liveness:** check the posting and its date before spending evaluation time.
- **Consistent evaluation:** apply the same fit rubric to every role so a polished description does not outweigh a missing hard constraint.
- **Tracking:** keep role identity, source, date, state, unresolved question and next action in one place.

Then keep the decisions that carry risk:

- A model score is a hypothesis about fit, not an employer's assessment. Re-read the posting and test the score against the hard constraints yourself.
- A liveness check is point-in-time. The employer's ATS remains the authority, as in Section 2.
- "Draft-only" must survive inspection. Auto-submit removes the review step that catches an invented metric or an ignored requirement; generated claims need the same fact check as your own CV.
- "Local-first" is not the same as private. The CV, reports and recruiter names are still personal data on disk, and the configured model provider may receive them. Keep the workspace in a private repository or ignored directory, never in a public repo, and read the provider boundary before the first run.
- Prefer exportable, human-readable files. If leaving the tool means extracting a proprietary database, the pipeline owns you.
- Pin the version and read the install path before running `npx` or a similar one-shot command; you are executing third-party code with access to your files.

The split is the same authority rule applied to automation: the tool finds, checks and organizes; you verify at the source, decide fit and submit.

## 7. Know What the Channel Cannot See

No board covers the whole market. Stealth companies, retained searches, internal transfers and roles filled through a network may never appear publicly. A quiet board can mean the company is not hiring, or that it hires through channels you cannot see. Use several channel types, keep the date beside every claim, and stop treating an old company list as a set of openings.

The practical test is simple: can you open the employer's current posting, identify the role and team, and state what you still need to confirm? If not, the record is discovery material, not an application target.

## Summary

Discover companies through startup directories and communities, then verify the opening at the employer's ATS. Record the role identity, source, date, state and hard constraints before tailoring an application. Read startup roles for ownership, runway and equity terms rather than the label, and keep a small pipeline with dated next actions. Use a local job-search workspace for the repeatable discovery, liveness checks, scoring and tracking, but keep verification, fit judgment, fact-checking and submission with you. Channels improve discovery; only the employer establishes that a vacancy is current.

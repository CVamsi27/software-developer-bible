# Find Startup and Product Roles Without a Stale Job List

A startup role can open on Monday, collect 400 applications by Wednesday, and close before an aggregator even removes the listing. Most engineers waste hours applying to stale copies on generic boards instead of querying the sources where startups actually publish live openings.

A reliable senior search does two things well: it discovers funded teams through high-signal startup channels, and it verifies every vacancy directly on the employer's own Applicant Tracking System (ATS) before you invest an hour tailoring your resume.

```text
VC Portfolio Hubs & Startup Directories (high-signal discovery)
  ─► Direct ATS Search / Live API Query (verify role is actually open today)
       ─► Community or Referral (team context + faster review)
            ─► Dated Role Record (evaluate runway, equity & fit before applying)
```

## Definition

A **job channel** is a discovery path that surfaces companies and openings (such as a venture capital (VC) portfolio board, startup directory, or community thread). An **Applicant Tracking System (ATS)** — such as Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, or Personio — is the employer's own hiring system and the **single source of truth** for whether a role is still open. Every third-party job board is a cache of the ATS.

> **Textbook Definition:** A job search channel is a source or method that exposes employment opportunities; its usefulness depends on coverage, freshness, and the authority of the source that owns the vacancy.

## First Principles

Only the employer can open, modify, or close a requisition. Every aggregator, newsletter, or scraped spreadsheet is a **stale cache** that ages independently — it can keep showing a filled role for weeks or miss a newly opened senior role altogether.

Instead of chasing a static "top 100 startups hiring" list that rots in a month, build a repeatable workflow that queries **live employer ATS boards** and **VC-backed startup hubs**. Then filter strictly for your hard constraints: location, visa or remote eligibility, stack, and compensation.

## 1. Where Real Startup Openings Live (By Market and Signal Quality)

Not all channels are equal. Start with **VC portfolio job boards** and **direct ATS search** — they have far higher signal-to-noise than mass aggregators because the companies listed actually raised funding and pull directly from their live ATS feeds.

| Channel type | Best used for | Authority | Common failure mode |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Employer ATS or careers page** | Confirming role exists, exact team, location, and application status | **Source of truth** | Poor discovery if you do not already know the company |
| **VC portfolio job boards** | Finding funded Seed–Series C startups hiring right now | Syncs from portfolio ATSs | Still confirm on the company's own ATS before tailoring |
| **Startup directories & niche boards** | Filtering by stage, equity, remote policy, or country | Copies employer data | Stale profiles and already-filled roles |
| **General aggregators** | Broad keyword scans across the whole market | Scraped or reposted | Duplicates, expired listings, and blind agency reposts |
| **Engineering communities & referrals** | Talking directly to hiring managers; unposted roles | Human context | Informal claims still need written confirmation |

### High-signal startup hubs (verified discovery channels)

Open these with date filters (`last 7–14 days`) and always click through to the employer's own ATS page:

- **VC Portfolio Job Boards (Global, United States (US), and European Union (EU) — highest signal):**
  - [Work at a Startup](https://www.workatastartup.com/) and [Y Combinator company directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies) — Y Combinator (YC)-backed startups filterable by batch, stage, remote, and stack.
  - [Sequoia Capital Jobs](https://jobs.sequoiacap.com/jobs) — live openings across Sequoia portfolio startups worldwide.
  - [Index Ventures Startup Jobs](https://www.indexventures.com/startup-jobs/) — strong mix of EU and US high-growth startups.
  - [Point Nine Jobs](https://jobs.pointnine.com/jobs) — focused heavily on European business-to-business Software as a Service (B2B SaaS) and developer-tool startups.
  - [a16z Portfolio Jobs](https://jobs.a16z.com/jobs) and [Accel Jobs](https://jobs.accel.com/jobs) — Seed through growth-stage engineering roles across the US, EU, and India.
- **EU & Germany Startup Ecosystems:**
  - [Welcome to the Jungle](https://www.welcometothejungle.com/en) — European product companies with detailed team profiles.
  - [Berlin Startup Jobs](https://berlinstartupjobs.com/), [GermanTechJobs](https://www.germantechjobs.de/), and [Arbeitnow](https://www.arbeitnow.com/) — German startup and English-speaking tech discovery (always confirm visa and work authorization on the employer's ATS).
- **Remote-First (EU, US, and Global):**
  - [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/jobs) — startup roles with company stage, equity, and remote filters.
  - [Remotive](https://remotive.com/) and [startup.jobs](https://startup.jobs/) — remote and global startup discovery.
  - [Hacker News "Who is hiring?"](https://hnhiring.com/) — monthly engineering-posted threads; often reaches a founder or staff engineer directly.
- **India Product Startups:**
  - [HasJob](https://hasjob.co/), [Cutshort](https://cutshort.io/), and [Instahyre](https://instahyre.com/) — Indian product and startup engineering roles.
- **Broad Market (use with strict date filters):**
  - [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/) and [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/) — wide reach, but expect reposts and expired listings; resolve every interesting role back to the employer's careers page.

## 2. Query Employer ATS Boards Directly (Copy-Paste Recipes)

Most Seed–Series C startups host their live openings on four ATS platforms: **Ashby**, **Greenhouse**, **Lever**, and **Personio** (especially common in Germany and the EU). Searching those domains directly bypasses aggregators and lands you straight on the employer's live requisition.

Use these Google search templates and set **Tools → Past week** (or **Past month**) to surface fresh openings:

```text
# EU / Germany Senior Full-Stack roles on Ashby & Greenhouse
site:jobs.ashbyhq.com ("Senior Full Stack" OR "Senior Software Engineer") ("Berlin" OR "Munich" OR "Germany" OR "Europe") ("TypeScript" OR "React" OR "Node")
site:boards.greenhouse.io ("Senior Full Stack" OR "Senior Software Engineer") ("Berlin" OR "Amsterdam" OR "Germany") ("TypeScript" OR "PostgreSQL")

# German startups using Personio
site:jobs.personio.com ("Senior Full Stack" OR "Senior Backend" OR "Senior Frontend") ("TypeScript" OR "Node" OR "React")

# Remote-first EU / Global startups on Ashby & Lever
site:jobs.ashbyhq.com ("Senior Full Stack" OR "Founding Engineer") ("Remote") ("TypeScript" OR "Next.js")
site:jobs.lever.co ("Senior Full Stack" OR "Senior Software Engineer") ("Remote") ("TypeScript" OR "React")

# India product startups on Greenhouse & Lever
site:boards.greenhouse.io ("Senior Software Engineer" OR "Senior Full Stack") ("Bengaluru" OR "Bangalore" OR "Hyderabad" OR "India") ("TypeScript" OR "Node")
site:jobs.lever.co ("Senior Software Engineer" OR "Full Stack") ("Bengaluru" OR "India" OR "Remote")
```

**Why this works so well:** Every hit is a real requisition Uniform Resource Locator (URL) (`jobs.ashbyhq.com/<company>/<job-id>`, `boards.greenhouse.io/<company>/jobs/<id>`, `jobs.lever.co/<company>/<id>`, or `<company>.jobs.personio.com/job/<id>`). If a link returns HTTP `404` or "position filled," you know immediately — zero wasted effort.

## 3. Resolve the Posting and Capture Seven Facts Before Tailoring

Before writing a custom cover letter or adjusting your resume, open the employer's own ATS page and log seven concrete facts in your private tracker:

| Field | What to verify and record |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Employer & legal entity** | Official careers domain, legal entity name, and country (subsidiaries and acquisitions change hiring entities) |
| **Role identity** | Exact title, job or requisition identifier (ID), owning team, and seniority level |
| **Source & check date** | Direct ATS URL plus the exact date you confirmed it was live |
| **State** | `Open`, `Closed`, `Reposted`, or `Uncertain` |
| **Hard constraints** | Work authorization or visa sponsorship, hybrid versus remote country list, on-call, and contract versus full-time |
| **Compensation** | Published salary band or written recruiter figure with currency (base versus equity separated) |
| **Next action** | One specific step (e.g., ask recruiter about Blue Card support, or message referral) with a date |

When an aggregator and the employer's ATS disagree, the **ATS wins for whether the role exists**, and **written recruiter confirmation wins for specific hiring conditions**. Never assume "remote" means "work from any country" or "English-speaking" means "sponsors relocation."

## 4. Evaluate a Startup Role: Ownership, Runway, and Worked Equity Math

"Startup" spans a 4-person pre-seed team with 10 months of cash and a 400-person Series C company preparing for profitability. Read past the title and evaluate three realities: **scope**, **runway**, and **real equity value**.

### Concrete questions to ask the founder or hiring manager

- **Scope & urgency:** *"Why is this role open right now — new product bet, team growth, or backfill — and what will I own in my first 90 days?"*
- **Decision authority:** *"What technical decisions can a senior engineer here ship without committee sign-off?"*
- **Runway & business health:** *"What is your current funding stage and runway, and what revenue or product milestones unlock the next round?"*
- **Operational load:** *"Who is on call, how often does it page, and how do you balance feature delivery with reliability?"*
- **Work authorization:** *"Does this specific requisition support hiring someone who requires work authorization or relocation?"*

Watch out for **title inflation**: a "Founding Engineer" at a seed startup may own frontend, backend, infrastructure, and customer support, while a "Senior Engineer" at a Series C company may own one microservice. Compare actual daily scope, mentorship, and total cash against your goals.

### How to read startup equity (worked example)

A bare percentage (`"0.10% equity"`) or share count (`"10,000 options"`) is **not** comparable to salary until you know five numbers: **grant size**, **total diluted shares (or valuation)**, **strike price**, **vesting schedule and cliff** (commonly 4 years with a 1-year cliff), and **liquidation preference**.

Here is an illustrative calculation for a realistic Series A offer:

```text
Illustrative offer:
  • Base salary:         €95,000 per year
  • Options or VSOP:     0.10% of company at €40M post-money valuation
  • Paper grant value:   0.001 × €40,000,000 = €40,000 over 4 years (€10,000/yr gross)
  • Strike price cost:   Suppose strike price = 25% of current preferred value (€10,000 total)
  • Post-Series B/C dilution: ~30% dilution reduces 0.10% ─► ~0.07%

Now model three realistic outcomes (assuming 1x non-participating liquidation preference, €15M raised):
  1. Flat / down exit (€15M): Investors take their €15M preference first ─► common/options = €0.
  2. Modest exit (€80M):      0.07% × €80M = €56,000 − €10,000 strike = €46,000 pre-tax (~€11.5k/yr).
  3. Strong exit (€250M):     0.07% × €250M = €175,000 − €10,000 strike = €165,000 pre-tax (~€41k/yr).
```

**Takeaway:** Treat early-stage equity as a skewed upside bet, never as guaranteed cash. Verify whether the plan uses an **Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP)** (real shares or options with exercise tax and a post-termination exercise window — often 90 days unless extended) or a **Virtual Stock Option Plan (VSOP)** (virtual shares common in Germany, paid in cash on exit with no upfront exercise cost).

Compare recurring base salary first, then price equity conservatively across downside and base-case exits.

## 5. Verify Legitimacy Before Investing Time

Treat unsolicited job messages or sketchy listings as claims to verify:

- The exact opening exists on the employer's official ATS or careers domain.
- The recruiter's email domain matches the company, or an external agency explicitly names its client relationship.
- Nobody asks you to pay application fees, buy equipment up front, cash a check, or touch cryptocurrency.
- The recruiter and hiring manager describe the same interview stages and team.
- The company has a verifiable product, real customers or public filings, and a named legal entity.

Legitimate recruiting agencies often repost roles without naming the client up front — that alone is not fraud, just a prompt to verify the employer and date before investing deep prep time.

## 6. Automate Live Discovery and Liveness Checks Without Outsourcing Decisions

Repeatable scanning is a great fit for local scripts — **as long as you keep personal targets out of public repositories and keep every judgment call with you.**

### Query live ATS boards directly with `scripts/job_board_watch.py`

This repository includes [job_board_watch.py](../docs/scripts-map.md), which talks directly to public **Greenhouse**, **Ashby**, **Lever**, and **Personio** endpoints — the same public feeds a browser reads. Keep your board list JavaScript Object Notation (JSON) file in your private workspace (never in this public repo):

```bash
# 1. Discover live openings across your target startup ATS boards right now:
python3 scripts/job_board_watch.py --discover \
  --config ~/path/to/private/boards.json \
  --query "full stack,typescript,backend" \
  --location "berlin,remote,germany"

# 2. Watch applied roles for GONE (closed/ghosted) or REOPEN signals:
python3 scripts/job_board_watch.py --config ~/path/to/private/watches.json
```

For full local job-search workspaces (scanning boards, checking liveness, scoring roles against your curriculum vitae (CV), and drafting tailored applications), [career-ops](https://github.com/career-ops-hq/career-ops) is a public MIT-licensed example whose documented boundary is **draft-only**.

Use this clean division of labor with any job-search tool:

| Safe to automate | Always keep with you |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Discovery:** querying public ATS application programming interfaces (APIs) and deduplicating reposts | **Source verification:** opening the live ATS page before tailoring |
| **Liveness checks:** flagging closed or reopened postings | **Fit judgment:** checking hard constraints (visa, stack, level, pay) |
| **Consistent rubrics:** scoring roles against the same criteria | **Fact-checking drafts:** catching hallucinated metrics or unverified claims |
| **Tracker hygiene:** logging URLs, dates, and follow-up reminders | **Final submission:** reviewing and submitting every application yourself |

**Privacy & supply-chain boundary:** *"Local-first"* means your files stay on disk, **not** that nothing leaves your machine — any configured large language model (LLM) API receives the CV and prompts you send it. Keep CVs, target lists, and recruiter contacts in a private repository or gitignored directory, never a public repository, and pin versions before running `npx` or third-party scripts with filesystem access.

## 7. Run a Small Pipeline and Know What Boards Cannot See

No public board sees the entire market. Stealth startups, retained searches, internal transfers, and roles filled through founder networks never hit a careers page. Combine **two active channels** (direct ATS and VC boards plus targeted referrals) and review a small, focused pipeline weekly:

- **Close dead roles quickly** so you do not fixate on ghosted applications.
- **Make specific referral asks:** name the exact role URL, share two lines of relevant production evidence, and ask whether they can refer you or introduce you to the hiring manager. Never ask a stranger to *"review my resume and let me know if anything fits."*
- **Apply the practical readiness test:** can you open the employer's live posting today, name the team and product, and list the two open questions you need to confirm in the recruiter screen? If not, treat it as discovery material, not an application target.

## Summary

Discover funded startups through VC hubs, directories, and direct ATS search (`Ashby`, `Greenhouse`, `Lever`, `Personio`), verifying every role on the employer's ATS before investing time. Record seven dated facts, evaluate equity via ownership, runway, dilution, and liquidation preferences, and automate ATS checks privately while keeping verification and final submission manual.

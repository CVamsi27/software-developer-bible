## Communication Skills for Senior Engineer Interviews

Source: [Communication Skills for Senior Engineer Interviews](00.01-communication.md)

**Q:** An interviewer challenges your design halfway through. What should your response establish before defending it?

**A:** Clarify which requirement or assumption changed, explain the consequence, and revise the decision or defend its tradeoff with evidence. Do not treat pushback as a personal contest.

**Q:** How should you test whether a resume supports the target role?

**A:** Ask whether each prominent claim answers that role's requirements and whether a reader can find your scope, evidence and contribution quickly. Tailor truthful emphasis rather than copying unsupported keywords.

## Resume Tips for Senior Full Stack Engineers

Source: [Resume Tips for Senior Full Stack Engineers](00.02-resume-tips.md)

**Q:** How can an impact bullet remain credible when you lack a precise percentage?

**A:** Describe the change, scope and observable outcome with verifiable evidence. Use counts, adoption or a concrete before/after if available; never invent a metric to satisfy a template.

## STAR Method: Complete Guide for Senior Engineers

Source: [STAR Method: Complete Guide for Senior Engineers](00.03-star-method.md)

**Q:** Your STAR story sounds like a team biography. What is missing?

**A:** The action should show your own decisions and contributions while crediting collaborators. The result should connect those actions to an outcome and include what you learned.

## HR Round Strategy for Senior Full Stack Engineers

Source: [HR Round Strategy for Senior Full Stack Engineers](00.04-hr-questions.md)

**Q:** Why should a sign-on bonus be separated from an offer's recurring compensation?

**A:** It is a one-time payment and may have repayment conditions. Compare recurring cash, equity assumptions, benefits and first-year extras separately before evaluating the offer.

# Strategy — Revision

Read the section's learning chapters first. Close them, explain each answer with a concrete failure or tradeoff, then use the source link to check what you missed.

## Communication Skills for Senior Engineer Interviews

Source: [Communication Skills for Senior Engineer Interviews](00.01-communication.md)

**Q:** An interviewer challenges your design halfway through. What should your response establish before defending it?

**A:** Clarify which requirement or assumption changed, explain the consequence, and revise the decision or defend its tradeoff with evidence. Do not treat pushback as a personal contest.

## Resume Tips for Senior Full Stack Engineers

Source: [Resume Tips for Senior Full Stack Engineers](00.02-resume-tips.md)

**Q:** How should you test whether a resume supports the target role?

**A:** Ask whether each prominent claim answers that role's requirements and whether a reader can find your scope, evidence and contribution quickly. Tailor truthful emphasis rather than copying unsupported keywords.

**Q:** A resume says you “led architecture,” but the scope is unclear. What evidence would make the claim credible for a senior role?

**A:** Name the system boundary, scale or risk, decisions you owned, teams you aligned and the operational outcome. Support the result with measurements or another verifiable signal, and be ready to explain your contribution separately from the team's.

**Q:** How can an impact bullet remain credible when you lack a precise percentage?

**A:** Describe the change, scope and observable outcome with verifiable evidence. Use counts, adoption or a concrete before/after if available; never invent a metric to satisfy a template.

## STAR Method: Complete Guide for Senior Engineers

Source: [STAR Method: Complete Guide for Senior Engineers](00.03-star-method.md)

**Q:** Your STAR story sounds like a team biography. What is missing?

**A:** The action should show your own decisions and contributions while crediting collaborators. The result should connect those actions to an outcome and include what you learned.

## Find Startup and Product Roles Without a Stale Job List

Source: [Find Startup and Product Roles Without a Stale Job List](00.05-find-startup-roles.md)

**Q:** A role appears on an aggregator but not on the employer's careers page. What does that establish?

**A:** It establishes that an intermediary published a listing, not that the employer still owns an open vacancy. Treat the employer's ATS as the authority for existence and state, and use the aggregator only as discovery. Ask the recruiter when the mismatch matters, and record the check date.

**Q:** Why is a funding announcement insufficient evidence that a startup can fund a role through its expected hiring cycle?

**A:** A funding announcement describes a transaction at one date, not current cash, obligations, runway or this team's budget. Ask about the role's funding source and expected duration, and separate the company-wide total from the conditions of the actual opening.

**Q:** What makes an equity grant comparable across two startup offers?

**A:** The grant needs a valuation, strike price, vesting schedule, cliff, preferred-share stack and liquidity assumptions before it can be compared. A percentage alone is not cash. Model the downside and the likely exit range, then compare the result with recurring salary and bonus.

**Q:** A local job-search tool offers to scan boards, score roles and submit applications. Which parts should you keep?

**A:** Automate discovery, liveness checks, consistent scoring and tracker maintenance. Keep verification at the employer's ATS, the fit decision, fact-checking of every generated claim and the final submission. Auto-submit removes the review step that catches a fabricated metric or an ignored requirement.

**Q:** The tool says "local-first." What privacy question does that leave open?

**A:** Local-first says where the files live, not who receives them. The CV, reports and recruiter names remain personal data on disk, and the configured model provider may receive the content. Keep the workspace in a private repository or ignored directory, read the provider boundary, and pin the version before running third-party code.

## HR Round Strategy for Senior Full Stack Engineers

Source: [HR Round Strategy for Senior Full Stack Engineers](00.04-hr-questions.md)

**Q:** Why should a sign-on bonus be separated from an offer's recurring compensation?

**A:** It is a one-time payment and may have repayment conditions. Compare recurring cash, equity assumptions, benefits and first-year extras separately before evaluating the offer.

## Summary

Prepare evidence of your contribution, explain the decision clearly, and compare employment terms using their actual conditions. Verify that an opening is current at its owner before investing in it. Use tooling for repeatable discovery and tracking, but keep verification, fit judgment and submission with you. Revisit the source when a claim, story or offer assumption cannot survive a follow-up.

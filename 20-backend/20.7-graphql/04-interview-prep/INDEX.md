## Practice passes

1. Complete the [fundamentals revision](../01-fundamentals/REVISION.md), [execution revision](../02-execution/REVISION.md) and [client/production revision](../03-clients-and-production/REVISION.md) before timing answers.
2. Work through [all 50 authored questions](20.7.4.01-interview-questions.md). First explain the mechanism, then give an example and a failure boundary. Use domain-design prompts to discuss invariants, authorization, consistency and recovery.
3. Choose follow-ups that match the role: resolver/storage behavior for back-end work, cache and UI transitions for front-end work, and ownership/deployment trade-offs for architecture work.
4. Revisit the source chapter when an answer relies on a slogan, unsupported performance claim or undocumented library API. Readiness comes from explaining and adapting the mechanism, not completing a question count.

## Experience boundary

The [Docita case study](../../../projects/docita/INDEX.md) supplies source-backed REST/domain comparisons. It does not establish that you evaluated or shipped GraphQL. State a GraphQL design as a proposal unless you have actual implementation and validation evidence; do not reuse a fabricated “we evaluated it” interview story.

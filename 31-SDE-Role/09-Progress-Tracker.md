## 🏆 Interview Day Preparation

### One Week Before

- [ ] Review cheat sheet daily
- [ ] Do 2 mock interviews
- [ ] Polish all behavioral stories
- [ ] Review weak areas
- [ ] Test video/audio setup
- [ ] Research interviewers on LinkedIn

### Day Before

- [ ] Light review only (1-2 hours max)
- [ ] Prepare outfit/clothes
- [ ] Set up workspace
- [ ] Get good sleep (8+ hours)
- [ ] Prepare questions for interviewers

### Day Of

- [ ] Review notes (30 min max)
- [ ] Eat healthy breakfast
- [ ] Arrive/log in early
- [ ] Stay calm and confident
- [ ] Remember: You've prepared for this!

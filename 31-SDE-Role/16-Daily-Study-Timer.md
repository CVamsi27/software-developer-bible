
## ⏰ Recommended Daily Schedule

### 4-Hour Study Day

```text

08:00 - 08:05  Set up workspace, start timer
08:05 - 08:30  🍅 Pomodoro 1: Review yesterday's material
08:30 - 08:35  ☕ Break
08:35 - 09:00  🍅 Pomodoro 2: Solve new problem 1
09:00 - 09:05  ☕ Break
09:05 - 09:30  🍅 Pomodoro 3: Solve new problem 2
09:30 - 09:35  ☕ Break
09:35 - 10:00  🍅 Pomodoro 4: Solve new problem 3
10:00 - 10:15  🎉 Long Break

10:15 - 10:40  🍅 Pomodoro 5: Learn new pattern
10:40 - 10:45  ☕ Break
10:45 - 11:10  🍅 Pomodoro 6: Pattern practice problems
11:10 - 11:15  ☕ Break
11:15 - 11:40  🍅 Pomodoro 7: System Design study
11:40 - 11:45  ☕ Break
11:45 - 12:10  🍅 Pomodoro 8: Behavioral prep
12:10 - 12:30  🎉 Long Break + Summary

```

**Total: 8 Pomodoros = 3.5 hours productive + 1 hour breaks = 4.5 hours total**

### 6-Hour Study Day

```text

Morning Session (3 hours):
├── 08:00 - 10:00  4 Pomodoros (Coding - New Problems)
├── 10:00 - 10:15  Long Break
└── 10:15 - 11:00  2 Pomodoros (Pattern Learning)

Afternoon Session (2.5 hours):
├── 14:00 - 15:30  3 Pomodoros (System Design + Behavioral)
├── 15:30 - 15:45  Long Break
└── 15:45 - 17:00  2 Pomodoros (Review + Weak Areas)

Evening Session (1 hour):
└── 18:00 - 19:00  2 Pomodoros (Light Review + Planning)

Total: 13 Pomodoros = 5.5 hours productive + 1 hour breaks = 6.5 hours total

```

## 💡 Tips for Effective Pomodoro Sessions

### Do's ✅
- **Set a clear goal** for each pomodoro before starting
- **Turn off notifications** — phone, email, Slack
- **Use a physical timer** — more accountability than phone
- **Stand up during breaks** — stretch, move around
- **Track every session** — even short ones count
- **Review at end of day** — what worked, what didn't

### Don'ts ❌
- **Don't check phone** during work sessions
- **Don't skip breaks** — they prevent burnout
- **Don't multitask** — one pomodoro = one task
- **Don't extend sessions** — stick to 25 minutes
- **Don't skip tracking** — data helps you improve
- **Don't study past your limit** — quality > quantity

### Pomodoro Customization

| Option | Work | Short Break | Long Break | Best For |
|--------|------|-------------|------------|----------|
| Classic | 25 min | 5 min | 15 min | Standard study |
| Extended | 45 min | 10 min | 20 min | Deep focus work |
| Short | 15 min | 3 min | 10 min | Review sessions |
| Intensive | 50 min | 10 min | 30 min | System design |

## 📈 Productivity Metrics

### Weekly Productivity Score

```text

Formula: (Productive Minutes / Total Minutes) × 100

Target: 70-80% productive time

Example:
- Total session time: 6 hours (360 min)
- Productive time: 5 hours (300 min)
- Break time: 1 hour (60 min)
- Productivity score: (300/360) × 100 = 83% ✅

```

### Efficiency Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Pomodoros/Day** | Total pomodoros / Days studied | 8-12 |
| **Problems/Pomodoro** | Problems solved / Pomodoros | 0.5-1 |
| **Avg Time/Problem** | Total time / Problems solved | 20-30 min |
| **Review Ratio** | Review time / Total time | 30-40% |
| **Focus Score** | Completed pomodoros / Planned pomodoros | 80%+ |

---

> **Remember:** The goal isn't to study more hours — it's to study MORE EFFECTIVELY. Quality focused time beats distracted hours every time!

> **Pro Tip:** Track your pomodoros for 2 weeks, then analyze the data. You'll discover your most productive times of day, optimal session lengths, and topics that need more attention.

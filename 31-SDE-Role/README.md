## 🎯 Readiness Checklist

### Technical Skills
- [ ] Can solve Medium problems in 20-25 minutes
- [ ] Can solve Hard problems in 35-45 minutes
- [ ] Can identify patterns within minutes
- [ ] Can explain solution clearly while coding
- [ ] Can analyze time/space complexity accurately

### System Design
- [ ] Can design scalable systems in 45 minutes
- [ ] Can discuss trade-offs confidently
- [ ] Can reference specific technologies (AWS/Azure/GCP)
- [ ] Can handle scaling questions
- [ ] Can discuss monitoring and observability

### Behavioral
- [ ] Have 8-10 polished STAR stories
- [ ] Can answer "Why [Company]?" authentically
- [ ] Can demonstrate company values in every story
- [ ] Practiced with mock interviews
- [ ] Comfortable with behavioral questions

### Company-Specific
- [ ] Read the company-specific guide
- [ ] Know the interview process and format
- [ ] Understand the company's values/culture
- [ ] Can reference specific products/technologies
- [ ] Have questions prepared for interviewers

## 💡 Tips for Success

1. **Consistency > Intensity** — 4 hours daily for 12 weeks beats 12 hours daily for 4 weeks
2. **Practice Explaining** — Interviewers care about HOW you think, not just WHAT you know
3. **Mock Interviews** — Do at least 10 before your real interviews
4. **Company Research** — Tailor your prep to the specific company and team
5. **Behavioral Matters** — It's 50% of the interview; prepare STAR stories early
6. **Track Progress** — Use the Progress Tracker to stay motivated and identify gaps
7. **Rest Before Interview** — Don't cram the night before; trust your preparation

## 🏆 Final Milestone Checklist

Before scheduling your interviews, you should be able to:

- [ ] Solve most Medium LeetCode problems in **20-30 minutes**
- [ ] Solve common Hard problems in **35-45 minutes**
- [ ] Identify the correct algorithmic pattern within a few minutes
- [ ] Explain your solution, edge cases, and complexity clearly while coding
- [ ] Design scalable systems and discuss trade-offs confidently
- [ ] Answer core CS questions on OS, networking, databases, and OOP
- [ ] Defend every line of your resume and discuss design decisions behind your projects
- [ ] Handle behavioral questions with structured, specific STAR examples

# Interview Lanes

> **Two peer interview preparation lanes** that draw from the [7 study stacks](../00-Interview-Strategy/). Pick yours before diving in.

| Lane | Folder | For | Style |
|------|--------|-----|-------|
| **Indian SDE / FAANG** | [81-Indian-SDE-FAANG/](81-Indian-SDE-FAANG/) | Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta, Apple, in-country India product companies, US H1B/STEM-OPT | LeetCode-heavy, 8-12 week intensive, USD 150-400K+ |
| **Abroad Full-Stack** | [82-Abroad-Full-Stack/](82-Abroad-Full-Stack/) | EU / non-Indian mid-stage startups (n8n, SumUp, Scalable, sennder, THRYVE, Peter Park, Hostaway, GoGlobal) | Take-home + system design, 4-week plan, EUR 70-110K + visa sponsorship |

**Not sure?** Read the [lane decision matrix in 82-Abroad-Full-Stack](82-Abroad-Full-Stack/INDEX.md). If you're in between (e.g., Booking.com, Spotify, Stripe, remote-first US), use both lanes.

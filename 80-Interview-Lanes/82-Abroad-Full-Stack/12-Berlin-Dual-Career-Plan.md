## Why Berlin (Locked Decision)

- **Both of you can land English-only jobs in Berlin.** That was the question that decided the country. Vamsi's full stack is English-default in Berlin startups. Wife's CSSA technical delivery is English-friendly at Allianz Munich (in-house), DKB Code Factory (live postings), BlueRose (Indian SI with Munich office), PegaOne (explicitly English), Accenture EU Pega Practice (flagship banking, "English B2+, German is a plus").
- **Free Kita from 1yo** = €15,000–20,000/yr saved vs Dublin (£1,200/mo) or London (£1,500/mo).
- **EU Blue Card** for Vamsi (€48,300/yr IT shortage threshold, easily cleared) + family reunification for wife.
- **PR in 21 months** (with B1 German for wife) → **EU citizenship in 6–8 years.**
- **Berlin housing painful but manageable** (€1,500–2,000/mo for 3-bed Pankow/Friedrichshain, vs €2,500–3,000 in Dublin/London).
- **No other EU country gives both of you English-only roles in your respective fields at the same scale.** Netherlands wants Dutch for Pega. Switzerland impossible sponsorship. Ireland = housing kills savings.

**Plan B (Dublin, Ireland):** Same dual-career English-only profile works, but Dublin housing + childcare cost €20k+/yr more than Berlin. Keep Dublin warm as fallback only.

### Why the CSSA English-only market is bigger than expected

**The crucial distinction most candidates miss:** Pega jobs in Germany split into two tiers:
1. **Business Architect (BA)** — client workshops, requirement gathering, stakeholder-facing → C1 German required.

**Wife's profile is Tier 2.** That's ~35% of the German Pega market hireable in English. B1 opens ~60%, B2 opens ~85%.

## Tax Class Strategy (Important)

When wife arrives on family reunification, you should be on **Steuerklasse III** (most favorable for higher earner), wife on **V** (no income yet). After she starts working, switch both to **IV/IV** (default for married couples both working).

- **III/V before she works:** You pay less tax, more take-home
- **IV/IV after she works:** Equal tax treatment
- **Free money:** €3,000–5,000/yr in your pocket from this switch alone

File annual tax return (Steuererklärung) via ELSTER or with a Steuerberater (€200–500/year, saves you €1,000+ in refunds).

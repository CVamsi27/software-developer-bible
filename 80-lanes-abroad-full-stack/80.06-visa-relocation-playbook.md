# Visa & Relocation Playbook — Eight European Countries

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Specialized-lightgrey)](../docs/topic-importance.md)



A job offer, immigration permission and a workable household budget are three
separate conditions. Resolve all three before choosing a destination. This
chapter compares routes; the [compensation guide](80.05-comp-eu-benchmarking.md)
covers budgets, and the [personal relocation plan](personal/germany-relocation-bible.md)
contains the household's actual decisions.

## First Principles

1. **A residence route has eligibility conditions, not a guaranteed timeline.** Salary, qualifications, employer status and family applications can block a move independently of the offer.
2. **Permission to enter, reside and work are different decisions.** A visitor visa does not establish work rights, and a spouse's rights depend on their own residence status.
3. **Relocation expenses precede the first salary.** Housing deposits, insurance, travel and delayed appointments require accessible funds even when an employer reimburses costs later.

## The 8-Country Visa Matrix

The figures below were checked against official sources on **2026-10-01**.
They are route-specific eligibility thresholds, not recommended salaries or
processing-time promises. Six destinations are EU members; the UK and
Switzerland use separate systems. Recheck the authority's table for the
application date and actual occupation.

### Netherlands

The Highly Skilled Migrant route normally uses an IND-recognized sponsor.
The 2026 gross monthly thresholds, excluding holiday allowance, are **€5,942
for applicants aged 30 or over** and **€4,357 below 30**; a separate reduced
criterion applies to eligible graduates. [IND income requirements](https://ind.nl/en/required-amounts-income-requirements)

Tax treatment is a separate application. The expat allowance is conditional,
not an automatic reduction of everyone's salary tax: the maximum remains 30%
in 2026, with 27% from 2027 subject to transition rules. [Dutch government expat scheme](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/inkomstenbelasting/belastingvoordeel-buitenlandse-werknemers)

### Germany

For the EU Blue Card, the 2026 annual gross thresholds are **€50,700 normally**
and **€45,934.20 for eligible shortage occupations/new entrants**, with approval
requirements for the reduced route. An eligible qualification or relevant IT
experience and a qualifying job offer are still necessary. The employer need
not hold a UK-style sponsor licence, but the application is not merely an
offer letter. Settlement can be available after **27 months with A1 German**,
or **21 with B1**, when the other conditions are satisfied. [Official Blue Card guide](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/eu-blue-card)

#### Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card)

This is a job-search route, not permission to start any full-time job. An
eligible recognized skilled worker can use the recognition route; otherwise
the points route needs its baseline qualification/language conditions and at
least six points. Recognition of a university alone does not prove the
specific degree is equivalent. [Official route and self-check](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card)

For 2026, proof of subsistence is **€1,091 per month**: a blocked account,
declaration of commitment, or qualifying part-time income can support the
application. A blocked account is therefore a legitimate Opportunity Card
mechanism; it is not a universal Blue Card requirement. A lower qualifying
part-time income can be supplemented by blocked funds. [Official funding FAQ](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/questions-answers)

Check permitted working hours, trial-work conditions, insurance and family
eligibility before applying. Compare the funded job-search runway with the
alternative of interviewing from the current country; do not assume trial
work guarantees a permanent offer.

### Ireland

Critical Skills eligibility depends on the occupation, qualification and job
offer. The standard listed-occupation remuneration threshold is **€40,904**;
other routes and recent graduates have different thresholds. The higher
remuneration route uses **€68,911** under the current official rules. Stamp 4,
long-term residence and citizenship are separate statuses, not an automatic
"two years to permanent residence" promise. [Critical Skills requirements](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/critical-skills-employment-permit/)

### Sweden

There is a formal salary requirement. For applications covered by the new
rules from 1 June 2026, pay must reach 90% of the applicable median salary and
meet the profession's collective agreement or usual terms. The current
published threshold is **SEK 34,470 monthly**. Exceptions and transitional
extension rules are route-specific. [Migration Agency salary requirements](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/word-explanations/salary-requirements-for-a-work-permit.html)

### Finland

The 2026 specialist/EU Blue Card salary threshold is **€3,937 gross monthly**.
[Official income requirements](https://migri.fi/en/working-in-finland/income-requirement)
Permanent residence is not uniformly available after four years: applications
from 8 January 2026 follow different paths, including a six-year route with
work-history and language conditions. [Permanent-residence language/path requirements](https://migri.fi/en/language-skills-requirement)

### United Kingdom

The Skilled Worker route requires an approved sponsor. Normally pay must meet
**both £41,700 annually and the occupation's going rate**, using the higher
requirement; discounts and transitional routes have their own conditions.
"IT is a shortage occupation" does not establish a universal £33,000 salary
floor. [UK Skilled Worker salary rules](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/your-job)

### Switzerland

For non-EU/EFTA applicants, employer sponsorship, admission criteria, labour
market precedence and quotas matter. There is no universal CHF 95,000 legal
salary threshold or automatic five-year B permit for every applicant.
[Swiss admission criteria](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/themen/arbeit/nicht-eu_efta-angehoerige/grundlagen_zur_arbeitsmarktzulassung.html)

### Austria

The Red-White-Red Card has several eligibility routes. For **Other Key
Workers**, the 2026 minimum is **€3,465 gross monthly**, with a labour-market
test and at least 55 points. Do not apply that figure to every card category.
[Official Other Key Workers route](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/permanent-immigration/other-key-workers/)

## The Family-Driven Decision Framework

Compare a small number of viable cities using evidence rather than an invented
score such as "25/25":

1. **Both careers:** current vacancies, language requirements and each partner's work rights.
2. **Schooling:** actual admission availability, language support, fees and commute. International school is an option, not a universal requirement; local public school may be suitable.
3. **Budget:** net income scenarios, housing deposit, childcare, insurance and a delayed-start reserve. Tax concessions belong in a conditional scenario.
4. **Route dependencies:** recognized qualifications, sponsor eligibility, family documents and appointment availability.

Keep employer promises and household choices in the canonical personal plan,
with the date and source of each assumption.

## Relocation Package — What to Negotiate

Request a written breakdown of immigration support, family travel, temporary
housing, shipping, tax advice and reimbursements. These are negotiated benefits,
not standard legal entitlements. Confirm caps, payment timing, taxable treatment
and any repayment clause if employment ends early. Economy versus business
flights, school-search assistance and partner-career support vary by offer.

## Common Pitfalls

- Signing a contract may be necessary for the visa application; use a lawful-start condition rather than insisting on approval before any signature.
- An employer's relocation reimbursement does not remove the need to fund deposits and the period before reimbursement.
- Permanent residence, citizenship and work rights are separate questions. A residence-year count alone does not establish eligibility.
- A processing estimate is not an appointment or decision date. Avoid irreversible bookings based on a fixed "90-day relocation" promise.

## The 90-Day Relocation Timeline

Use 90 days as a planning scenario, then schedule dependencies from the real
appointments: qualify the route → obtain the contract/documents → apply →
receive the relevant permission → travel/start lawful work → register locally
and complete residence/family steps. Housing and schooling can run in parallel,
but paid bookings and employment starts depend on actual permission.

## Summary

- Compare route eligibility, both careers and household runway before salary alone.
- Verify salary thresholds for the specific occupation, route and application date.
- Treat tax allowances and relocation benefits as conditional offer terms.
- Sequence the move around permissions and appointments, not a guaranteed duration.

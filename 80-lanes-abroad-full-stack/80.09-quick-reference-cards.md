# Quick Reference — Find the Decision You Need

Thirty minutes before an interview or offer call is the wrong time to hunt through thirty files. Use this quick-reference router *after* you have studied the lane guides to jump straight to the checklist, script, or failure boundary you need right now.

## First Principles

A cheat card stays useful only when it stays short. Rather than duplicating full explanations (which quickly drift out of sync), this page maps each immediate interview situation to its single canonical guide and reminds you of the three checks most people forget under pressure.

## 2. Five-Minute Pre-Call Sanity Check

- **Before any technical round:** Test audio/mic/screen share, mute desktop notifications, bump editor font size, and confirm your actual notice period and work-authorization status.
- **Before submitting a take-home:** Re-run setup from a brand-new folder following your own README literally, and verify zero secrets are committed.
- **Before an offer or negotiation call:** Have your net take-home estimate, walk-away base floor, and one concrete priority lever written in front of you — never bluff a fake competing offer.

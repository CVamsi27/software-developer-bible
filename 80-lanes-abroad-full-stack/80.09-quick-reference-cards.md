# Quick Reference — Find the Decision You Need

Use this page after studying the underlying guide. It routes a last-minute check to its canonical home instead of repeating story scripts, company claims, and relocation rankings.

## First Principles

A reference card cannot teach every prerequisite without becoming another chapter. It must therefore help the reader identify the current decision and reach the canonical explanation, while leaving detailed mechanisms and practice in their own homes.

## Prepare the final handoff

For a take-home, verify the clean-checkout instructions and the employer's requested sharing method. For an interview, confirm the appointment, tools, and role, and prepare the relevant evidence notes. State availability from actual notice and immigration constraints rather than a default promise of two weeks.

For a negotiation, use a dated comparison and real alternatives; do not bluff or treat a counteroffer as mandatory. For relocation, use confirmed eligibility and actual costs rather than an invented city score.

## Summary

A reference page should help locate a decision quickly. Personal answer drafts remain in [the private workspace](personal/README.md); detailed explanations live in the linked guides.

# Weekly Tracker — Turn Evidence into the Next Action

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)



The [study plan](80.13-study-plan.md) organizes preparation. This tracker records what happened and what should change next. Keep the filled record and application outcomes in [the canonical personal workspace](personal/README.md), rather than creating another copy in an external tool repository.

## First Principles

Completed activities do not automatically demonstrate skill. A tracker needs both effort and an observable result: the mock attempted, the failure found, and whether a delayed reattempt succeeded. Hiring outcomes also depend on factors outside preparation, so self-scores cannot predict an interview date.

## Record one row per useful observation

| Date | Activity | Evidence | Gap | Next action | Recheck |
| ------- | --------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Example | Unseen booking design | Identified hold race; missed late payment | Recovery incomplete | Trace expiry and successful payment together | Next mock |
| Example | Coding variation | Works on positive values; fails negative case | Invalid window assumption | Derive prefix-sum alternative | Delayed reattempt |
| Example | Take-home rehearsal | Clean checkout requires local configuration | Handoff incomplete | Document sample config and repeat setup | Before submission |

These are hypothetical rows, not a personal progress record. Use a short evidence pointer, not confidential content or a long diary.

## Review effort and outcomes separately

At the weekly review, compare planned time with actual time, choose the largest observed gap, and schedule a bounded correction. Keep successful skills in spaced retrieval rather than repeating the same lesson daily. Adjust the workload for available time and fatigue.

For applications, track stages and dates in the existing private application tracker. Distinguish a submission, a recruiter response, a screen, and an offer. Small samples and delayed responses make conversion estimates noisy; five unanswered applications do not prove a technical-preparation failure.

## Use readiness evidence, not a universal score

A useful coding signal is an unseen variant solved with a valid invariant and tests. A useful design signal is an explained failure and recovery path. A useful behavioral signal is an accurate event you can defend under follow-up. A useful take-home signal is a complete required path within the stated limit and a reproducible handoff.

A self-score can record confidence, but “7/10” is not a hiring threshold. Apply when the role and evidence support it while continuing to address gaps; do not delay indefinitely for an arbitrary problem count or promise callbacks within a fixed number of weeks.

## Summary

Track evidence, choose one corrective action, and check whether it worked. Keep the schedule in the study plan and the actual campaign record in the personal workspace.

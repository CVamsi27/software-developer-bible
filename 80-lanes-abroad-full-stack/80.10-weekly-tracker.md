# Weekly Tracker — 4-Week Prep Plan

> **Track your prep week by week.** Copy this template, fill in your actual progress. Use the readiness score to decide when to start applying.

## TL;DR

- **4-week plan, 50-55 hours, 2-3 hours/day**
- **Each week has a clear deliverable.** Don't move on until the deliverable is met.
- **Readiness score** at the end: if 7+/10, start applying. If <7, spend another week on the weakest area.
- **Track in a Notion, Google Sheet, or a markdown file.** Just track.

See [study-plan.md](80.13-study-plan.md) for the full plan with daily breakdowns.

---

## Week 1 — Foundation (12-14 hours)

**Focus:** Stories + company mapping + first mock

### Daily breakdown

| Day | Time | Activity | Deliverable |
|-----|------|----------|-------------|
| Mon | 2-3h | Read [story-bank.md](80.12-story-bank.md). Pick 5 of 8 stories. Write out punchline-first for each. | 5 stories written |
| Tue | 2-3h | Practice the 5 stories out loud. Record yourself. Listen back. | 5 stories recorded |
| Wed | 2h | Add 3 more stories. Practice all 8. | All 8 stories practiced |
| Thu | 2h | Behavioral — write answers to "Why this company?" for 3 target companies | 3 "Why this company?" answers |
| Fri | 2h | Behavioral — write "Why are you leaving?" and "What are you looking for?" | 2 more answers |
| Sat | 2-3h | First mock interview (Pramp, interviewing.io, or a friend) | Mock done, notes captured |
| Sun | 1h | Review mock notes. Identify 2-3 weak areas. Plan week 2. | Week 2 plan written |

### Deliverable checklist

- [ ] 8 STAR+R stories practiced out loud
- [ ] 3 "Why this company?" answers written (60 sec each)
- [ ] "Tell me about yourself" at 60 sec, practiced
- [ ] 1 mock interview completed
- [ ] 2-3 weak areas identified for week 2

### Week 1 readiness (out of 10)

Rate yourself:
- [ ] Stories memorized: __/10
- [ ] Mock performance: __/10
- [ ] "Why [company]?" answers: __/10

**If 7+/10 across all: move to Week 2.**
**If <7 on stories: spend 2 more days on them before moving on.**

## When to extend the plan

Extend by 1-2 weeks if:

- Mock scores are <7
- System design is shaky
- DSA is <20 mediums
- You're not getting callbacks after 5+ applications

Don't extend indefinitely. Real interview feedback is more useful than prep. Send applications by end of week 4, even if you don't feel 100% ready.

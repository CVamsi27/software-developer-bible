# Story Bank — Organize Evidence Before Rehearsal

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)



A story bank stores a small set of real events you can adapt to behavioral prompts. This chapter explains the method. Personal narratives and their supporting records belong in the canonical private story bank.

## First Principles

Several interview prompts can probe the same event from different angles. A single accurate account is easier to maintain than separately embellished scripts. Reuse works only when the event demonstrates the competency being asked about; a large project is not automatically an example of conflict or influence.

## Build a record, then an answer

For each event, record the situation, your responsibility, your decision, its alternatives, the observable result, and the later lesson. Attach a private evidence pointer and a disclosure limit. Separate a production incident, a staging defect, and a hypothetical scenario.

Metrics need their denominator, period, and measurement method. A CV claim does not prove the detailed narrative around it. If you cannot support a percentage or customer count, use a bounded qualitative outcome. Do not turn a textbook mechanism into an event you claim to have performed.

## Map competencies without rewriting facts

Select an event for delivery, another for collaboration, another for an error or changed approach, and enough others to cover the confirmed loop. There is no universal requirement for exactly eight stories. Map prompts to the relevant evidence and leave gaps visible; a missing story is better than an invented one.

Prepare a short version for an initial answer and a deeper version for follow-ups. Keep the action and result consistent between them. Explain your own contribution and credit collaborators accurately.

## Validate technical details before delivery

A performance story should distinguish a measured symptom from its inferred cause. A migration story needs the actual consistency and rollback contract. A security story must distinguish controls implemented from an audit outcome or legal compliance. Technical follow-ups will expose contradictions such as claiming no downtime while describing a blocking maintenance operation.

The private draft bank contains corrections and evidence checks for the existing authored examples. Those drafts must be verified before being delivered as personal history.

## Summary

Store the facts once in the private bank, support their metrics, and adapt the explanation to the prompt. Rehearsal should improve clarity without changing the event.

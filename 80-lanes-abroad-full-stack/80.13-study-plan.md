# Four-Week Interview Preparation Plan

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)

An unrealistic "study 5 hours every night after work" plan collapses by Day 4. This is a sustainable **4-week, 10-hour/week** preparation cycle designed for working senior full-stack engineers preparing for EU and remote product-company loops. Treat it as a flexible template — confirm your target employer's actual interview stages first, and swap any week's focus to match an upcoming real round. Keep your actual personal schedule and application records strictly in your private workspace.

## Apply in Parallel — Do Not Wait Until Week 4

Once your resume is consistent and verified, start applying in **Week 1** using live ATS discovery ([Find Startup Roles](../00-strategy/00.05-find-startup-roles.md)). Recruiter screens often take 1–2 weeks to schedule anyway, and real screening calls immediately tell you which interview formats deserve the bulk of your study hours.

## First Principles

Your time budget is fixed (~10 hours/week). When a real take-home or live technical interview arrives, **replace** lower-priority study blocks inside that budget — never stack it on top of a 10-hour plan and burn out. Let real practice failures (missed invariants, shaky explanations, messy handoffs) drive what you study the following week.

## Pick One Sustainable Weekly Rhythm

A proven **10 hours/week** cadence (40 hours total): **five 1-hour weekday sessions + one 4-hour weekend deep block + 1 hour Sunday review**. Adjust the split around your real work and family schedule — consistency beats heroic weekend marathons.

## Week 1: Establish Stories, Expose Coding Gaps & Start Applying (10 hrs)

| Block | Hrs | Concrete deliverable |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ---: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Confirm target roles & apply to live ATS openings | 2 | 3–5 verified live roles logged with dated constraints ([Company Profiles](80.07-company-profiles.md)) |
| Build 5–7 verified stories in private story bank | 2 | Bullet facts, ownership, outcomes & disclosure bounds ([Story Bank](80.12-story-bank.md)) |
| Drill coding variations (not memorized templates) | 3 | Solve 3–4 problems across core families + log broken assumptions ([Tech Screen Patterns](80.02-tech-screen-patterns.md)) |
| Run 1 focused coding or behavioral mock | 2 | Concrete debrief naming top gap |
| Sunday review & Week 2 adjustment | 1 | Update [Weekly Tracker](80.10-weekly-tracker.md) |

## Week 2: Deliver One Clean Vertical Slice Under a Timebox (10 hrs)

| Block | Hrs | Concrete deliverable |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Time-boxed 4-hour practical build | 4 | Working end-to-end slice + risky-path tests + clean README ([Take-Home Playbook](80.01-take-home-playbook.md)) |
| Clean-checkout audit & targeted refactor | 2 | Fresh-folder run verification + fix top design/test weakness |
| Coding variations + delayed re-solve of Week 1 misses | 2 | Confirm failed invariants now stick cold |
| Behavioral follow-up probing | 1 | Practice 90-second STAR + deep follow-ups aloud |
| Sunday review | 1 | Log evidence & adjust Week 3 priorities |

## Week 3: Master System Design Invariants & Failure Recovery (10 hrs)

| Block | Hrs | Concrete deliverable |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Three timed 45-min design drills (closed-book) | 4 | Whiteboard invariant, QPS/storage math & crash recovery ([12 Cases](80.08-system-design-case-bank.md)) |
| Deep-dive study on your #1 weakest backend/data gap | 2 | Read canonical stack chapter + trace one real failure |
| Coding variation + edge-case testing | 1 | Clean implementation + complexity proof |
| Full system design mock & debrief | 2 | Observed feedback on clarity and failure reasoning |
| Sunday review | 1 | Select Week 4 high-ROI polish targets |

## Week 4: Full-Loop Rehearsal & Role-Specific Polish (10 hrs)

| Block | Hrs | Concrete deliverable |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---: | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Full mixed mock (behavioral + coding + design) | 2 | Verify earlier gaps closed on unseen prompts |
| Repair any remaining high-risk weakness | 2 | Targeted chapter review + changed-case recheck |
| Product & workflow prep for active loops | 3 | 1-page product prep sheets + tailored questions |
| Active applications & referral outreach | 2 | Verified ATS roles + targeted referral asks |
| Sunday review | 1 | Decide next cycle focus or recovery break |

## Rules for Replanning Without Guilt

- **Real interview or take-home lands?** Swap out generic practice blocks immediately so you respect both the employer's timebox and your weekly cap.
- **Missed two weekday sessions?** Never double up blindly into an exhausted midnight cram — trim lower-priority tasks and keep your highest-risk gap front and center.

## Summary

Commit to one realistic weekly hour budget, apply to live roles from Week 1, and cycle through coding invariants, a time-boxed practical build, system design failure recovery, and timed mocks. Replan every Sunday from concrete gaps logged in your tracker rather than forcing a rigid calendar.

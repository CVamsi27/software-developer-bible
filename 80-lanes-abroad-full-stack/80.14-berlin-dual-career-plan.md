# Berlin Dual-Career Planning — Verify the Dependencies

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Specialized-lightgrey)](../docs/topic-importance.md)



A dual-career move has several dependent decisions: each adult's work route, actual employment opportunities, household cash flow, housing, and childcare or schooling. This guide organizes those decisions. Current personal facts, target employers, applications, and dated relocation plans remain in [the canonical private workspace](personal/README.md).

## First Principles

One person's offer does not establish the other person's employment or the household's ability to move. Some commitments, such as a deposit or resignation, are costly to reverse. The plan should therefore expose dependencies and uncertainty before attaching dates or promised savings.

## Separate preferences from verified conditions

A city preference can guide research without proving that it is universally best. Compare Berlin and any alternative using actual offers, the exact employing entity, work-country permission, commute addresses, current housing quotes, and available care or school places. An English-language job advertisement does not establish that every client engagement uses English or that sponsorship is available.

For a specialized platform role such as Pega, confirm the opening's delivery versus stakeholder responsibilities, certification requirements, language expectations, and contract type. Do not turn a few profiles into a percentage of a national job market. An EU-remote job does not itself grant residence or employment permission in every EU country.

## Use milestones with prerequisites

```text
Confirmed role and route
        ↓
Contract and required application evidence
        ↓
Permits / entry and work conditions confirmed
        ↓
Arrival plan, temporary housing, insurance and registration
        ↓
Permanent housing and care arrangements
        ↓
Second career plan reviewed against actual permission and opportunities
```

Steps can overlap where permitted, but record who owns each task, its prerequisite, the source, and the next check. Employer paperwork still matters for a German Blue Card even when the process differs from sponsor licensing elsewhere. A signed contract can be required before applying; use its conditions and agreed start date rather than a blanket “never sign before approval” rule.

Do not forecast an offer, permit, dependant arrival, and second job in fixed calendar months as if they were booked. Keep a base case and a delayed case. The [route-specific guide](80.06-visa-relocation-playbook.md) is the canonical place for current visa thresholds and settlement distinctions.

## Model cash flow once

Begin with a payroll estimate that states tax year, household assumptions, and which insurance and social contributions have already been deducted. Subtract actual living costs without deducting the same payroll contribution twice. Separate one-time moving costs from recurring expenses.

A hypothetical monthly net income of €5,000 and living costs of €3,800 leaves €1,200 before irregular costs and reserves. If an additional income is delayed, do not budget it as guaranteed. Use sensitivity cases for rent, childcare, exchange rates, and job start dates; a five-year savings projection needs explicit assumptions, not automatic raises and an invented net-pay ratio.

A German tax-class change affects wage withholding and can affect replacement benefits; it does not by itself create a reduction in the final annual income-tax liability. The earlier “€3,000–5,000 free money” claim was wrong. [Bavarian tax authority](https://www.lfst.bayern.de/steuerinfos/haeufig-gestellte-fragen/lohnsteuerabzug-und-lohnsteuerermaessigung)

## Check childcare and schooling before choosing housing

Berlin's official guidance describes publicly funded care through a voucher, generally without childcare fees and with a €23 monthly meal contribution. Eligibility, available places, additional arrangements, and the specific provider still need checking. A funding entitlement does not guarantee your preferred bilingual place. [Berlin voucher guidance](https://www.berlin.de/sen/jugend/familie-und-kinder/kindertagesbetreuung/kita-gutschein/)

Munich has its own fee and funding rules; Berlin's policy cannot be copied to all Bavaria. Verify the actual provider and booking hours. [Munich childcare costs](https://stadt.muenchen.de/infos/kosten-kita-platz.html)

For schools, inspect current admission rules, age cutoffs, language requirements, catchment or special selection rules, and the actual campus. A nearby apartment does not guarantee admission to a named bilingual or international school. Compare the complete journey from home to both workplaces and care/school rather than invented neighborhood scores and universal walking times.

Long-term university fees, admissions, and a child's future interests cannot be fixed from a newborn's relocation plan. Keep options open and refresh them when the decision becomes relevant; do not promise a particular school-to-university chain or lifetime savings.

## Distinguish benefits and settlement rights

Parental leave and parental allowance are different. Do not assume a year of full employer salary or automatic benefit eligibility; check the actual household and employment situation. [Federal family portal](https://familienportal.de/familienportal/familienleistungen/elterngeld/faq/was-ist-elterngeld-124628)

German naturalisation is conditional, generally after five years of legal residence under current guidance. Minors may be included in a parent's application subject to the applicable conditions; an existing child does not automatically gain citizenship merely because a parent naturalises. Permanent residence for the Blue Card holder and for a spouse must also be assessed separately. [Official naturalisation guide](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/living-permanently/naturalisation)

Use current prices for recurring costs. The Deutschland-Ticket costs €63 per month in 2026, rather than the older €49 used in the previous budget. It covers the stated local/regional services, not every train. [Deutsche Bahn](https://int.bahn.de/en/offers/regional/deutschland-ticket/)

## Keep the execution record private and current

Store dated evidence and actual commitments in the private relocation record. Maintain employer targets in the existing target list and applications in the existing tracker. The [company-research guide](80.07-company-profiles.md) and [preparation plan](80.13-study-plan.md) describe reusable methods without duplicating those records.

Open-source work may demonstrate a relevant skill, but it does not establish a callback multiplier, guaranteed merge deadline, or offer probability. State actual contributions and verified project experience in applications; never use the older invented incident/uptime scripts as personal history.

## Summary

Choose a city from verified household constraints and actual opportunities. Sequence commitments by prerequisites, model delayed outcomes, and keep personal execution data in one canonical place.

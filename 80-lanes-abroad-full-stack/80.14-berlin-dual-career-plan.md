# Berlin Dual-Career Planning — Verify the Dependencies

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Specialized-lightgrey)](../docs/topic-importance.md)

Moving abroad as a dual-career household involves five interlocking moving parts: **two careers and work permits, realistic net cash flow, temporary and permanent housing, childcare or schooling, and long-term residence rights**. One person's job offer does not automatically unlock the other four.

This guide lays out the exact dependency order and official German cost and policy facts to verify before making irreversible commitments — keeping all personal household details strictly in your private workspace.

## First Principles

Some decisions (like resigning a job, breaking a lease, or wiring a 3-month apartment deposit) are expensive to undo. Sequence your move around **verified prerequisites** rather than optimistic calendar dates, and always stress-test your budget assuming the second income starts several months after arrival.

## 1. Test City and Career Fit Against Real Offers, Not Assumptions

Before locking onto Berlin (or comparing Berlin versus Munich or Amsterdam), check real conditions:
- Confirm exact **employing entity, work-country permission, and daily working language** for *both* partners' target roles (an English job ad does not guarantee English client work or relocation sponsorship, and a European Union ("EU-remote") role does not grant legal residence across every EU country).
- For specialized enterprise platforms (such as Pega or SAP), verify delivery versus client-facing German requirements, certifications, and permanent versus contract market depth directly from live job postings rather than guesses.

## 2. Follow the Milestone Dependency Chain

```text
1. Confirmed role + legal visa route (see Visa Playbook 80.06)
        │
        ▼
2. Signed contract (with visa-delay start clause) + apostilled civil & degree docs
        │
        ▼
3. Entry visas / work permissions approved for primary & spouse
        │
        ▼
4. Arrival, temporary furnished housing, health insurance & Anmeldung (registration)
        │
        ▼
5. Long-term apartment + Kita / school placement
        │
        ▼
6. Second-career search & start under confirmed dependent work permission
```

Keep both a **base timeline** and a **delayed timeline** (for embassy wait times or apartment hunting). Embassies usually require a signed contract *before* filing the EU Blue Card application — protect yourself with a written start-date flexibility clause rather than refusing to sign. See [Visa & Relocation Playbook](80.06-visa-relocation-playbook.md) for current 2026 statutory thresholds.

## 3. Model Household Cash Flow Once — Without Double-Counting

1. Start from a current-year German payroll calculator stating your exact tax class and household setup (public health, pension, and unemployment deductions are already subtracted from gross pay — do not subtract them a second time).
2. Subtract realistic recurring local costs (warm rent, transit, groceries, utilities, childcare, and schooling) and separate one-time moving costs (flights, deposits, furniture).
3. Example math: an illustrative **€5,000 per month net single income** minus **€3,800 living costs** leaves **€1,200 per month** before irregular expenses and savings. Never count an unconfirmed second salary as guaranteed rent coverage!
4. **German tax-class fact:** Switching tax classes (`III/V` versus `IV/IV`) changes *monthly withholding* (and can affect wage-replacement benefits like parental allowance), yet it does **not** reduce your final annual income-tax bill after filing. ([Bavarian tax authority](https://www.lfst.bayern.de/steuerinfos/haeufig-gestellte-fragen/lohnsteuerabzug-und-lohnsteuerermaessigung))
5. **2026 transit price:** The **Deutschland-Ticket** costs **€63 per month in 2026** and covers local and regional public transit across Germany (excluding long-distance Intercity-Express (ICE) and Intercity (IC) trains). ([Deutsche Bahn](https://int.bahn.de/en/offers/regional/deutschland-ticket/))

## 4. Check Childcare and Schooling Before Picking a Neighborhood

- **Berlin childcare (`Kita-Gutschein`):** Publicly funded daycare via Berlin's voucher system is generally fee-free aside from a **€23 per month meal contribution** — *however*, eligibility, waitlists, and finding an open bilingual spot still require early verification with specific providers. ([Berlin voucher guidance](https://www.berlin.de/sen/jugend/familie-und-kinder/kindertagesbetreuung/kita-gutschein/))
- **Munich differs:** Bavaria and Munich set their own fee schedules and subsidies — never copy Berlin's free-daycare (`Kita`) rules to another German state. ([Munich childcare costs](https://stadt.muenchen.de/infos/kosten-kita-platz.html))
- **Schools:** Check actual admission cutoffs, catchment rules, and full door-to-door commutes for *both* parents and children before signing a lease. Living near a bilingual school does not guarantee an open seat.

## 5. Understand German Benefits and Long-Term Settlement Rules

- **Parental leave versus parental allowance (`Elterngeld`):** Job-protected leave and cash allowance are separate rules — do not assume a full year at 100% salary without checking current eligibility and caps. ([Federal family portal](https://familienportal.de/familienportal/familienleistungen/elterngeld/faq/was-ist-elterngeld-124628))
- **Naturalization and permanent residence:** German citizenship is generally possible after **5 years of legal residence** when all language, income, and integration conditions are met; dependent spouses and minor children have distinct eligibility checks rather than automatic blanket grants. ([Official naturalisation guide](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/living-permanently/naturalisation))

## Summary

Plan a dual-career Berlin or German move around verified prerequisites — legal work rights for both partners, a single-income-safe net cash-flow budget, real daycare (`Kita`) and school availability, and accurate 2026 cost and legal facts — while keeping all personal household decisions and finances strictly in your private workspace.

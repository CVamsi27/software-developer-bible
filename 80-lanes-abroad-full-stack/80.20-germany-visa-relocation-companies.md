# Check German Employer Relocation Support

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Specialized-lightgrey)](../docs/topic-importance.md)



Evaluate a specific opening, not an assumed list of visa-friendly companies. A company can support one role's relocation and require existing work authorization for another. Keep current targets and recruiter answers in the [private canonical records](personal/README.md).

## First Principles

Permission to take a job and practical help relocating are separate questions. An immigration route has eligibility and document conditions; an employer’s support changes who prepares or pays for the work, not those legal conditions.

## Separate admission from employer support

The [visa playbook](80.06-visa-relocation-playbook.md) holds the route conditions and dated thresholds. A qualifying offer is one requirement; qualification, salary, employment duration, applicable approvals, and the admission process also matter. Do not reduce the Blue Card to an offer letter and degree or imply that eligibility is already work authorization. Consult the [official Blue Card requirements](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/eu-blue-card).

Employer support is a different question: who prepares employment documents, coordinates the process, pays fees, provides temporary accommodation, and accommodates a delayed start? “International team” and “English-speaking” answer none of those questions.

## Confirm four things early

| Question | Useful evidence |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Can this role hire a candidate without existing German work authorization? | Dated response about this opening and employing entity |
| Who handles the employment documents and process coordination? | Named owner and agreed responsibilities |
| What financial support is offered? | Written covered costs, caps, repayment terms, and timing |
| What happens if admission takes longer? | A start-date contingency rather than a fixed processing promise |

One concise recruiter question is enough to start: “Does this opening support hiring a candidate who needs German work authorization, and what documentation and relocation help can the employing entity provide?” Follow up on the exact route and package when appropriate.

An unfamiliar HR team is a planning constraint to resolve, not automatically proof of a bad employer. Remote-before-relocation work needs an actual legal and payroll arrangement; do not promise it simply because the role is remote.

## Check qualification evidence correctly

Anabin concerns foreign institutions and qualifications; it is not a list of recognized employers. For the degree route, an institution's H+ status alone is insufficient: the degree's comparability must also be established. When the relevant entries are missing, check the official process for a ZAB Statement of Comparability. See [academic qualification guidance](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/recognition/foreign-academic-qualifications).

Use the current mission's checklist for the actual application. Prepare complete required evidence rather than assuming an incomplete application will be accepted and repaired later. Keep identity documents and personal qualification records private.

## Plan dependencies, not a guaranteed ninety-day calendar

```text
role fit and employer support
  → qualifying offer and required evidence
  → actual application and decision
  → confirmed entry/work permission and start plan
  → housing, registration and household arrangements
```

Some preparation can proceed in parallel. Processing and appointment availability vary; a planning estimate is not an approval date. Family admission and work rights have their own conditions. Confirm costly travel and housing commitments against actual milestones and cancellation terms.

## Summary

Verify this opening's hiring ability, paperwork owner, package, and timing contingency. Keep route conditions in the visa guide and current employer answers in private records so neither becomes a stale duplicate.

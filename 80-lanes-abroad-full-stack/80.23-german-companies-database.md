# Maintain a German & EU Startup Role Research Database

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Specialized-lightgrey)](../docs/topic-importance.md)

A static spreadsheet called "Top 50 German Tech Companies" goes stale within weeks: teams freeze headcount, startups get acquired, and open senior roles close in days. What works instead is combining a **clean, dated role tracker** in your private workspace with **direct links to official employer Applicant Tracking System (ATS) boards** so you can check live openings in minutes.

Use [Find Startup Roles](../00-strategy/00.05-find-startup-roles.md) for venture capital (VC) portfolio hubs and ATS search queries, then log only verified active openings in your database.

## First Principles

Companies do not hire uniformly — **specific teams open specific requisitions**. Separating **company context** (product, domain, official ATS) from **role snapshots** (exact requisition ID, requirements, check date) and **personal applications** (submitted curriculum vitae (CV) version, recruiter contacts, interview feedback) keeps your pipeline accurate and prevents an outdated recruiter reply from misdirecting a future application.

### Automate live checks across your target boards

Keep your personal target board list in a JavaScript Object Notation (JSON) file inside your private workspace (never in this public repo), then query live open roles on demand using [scripts/job_board_watch.py](../docs/scripts-map.md):

```json
{
  "boards": [
    "ashby:n8n",
    "ashby:linear",
    "ashby:supabase"
  ]
}
```

```bash
python3 scripts/job_board_watch.py --discover \
  --config ~/path/to/private/eu-boards.json \
  --query "full stack,senior,backend,frontend,typescript" \
  --location "berlin,munich,germany,europe,remote"
```

## 2. Keep Three Records Cleanly Separated

```text
1. Company Record (official domain, product docs, ATS board URL)
     └─ 2. Role Opening Record (exact job ID, requirements, location, check date)
          └─ 3. Private Application Record (submitted CV, contacts, status, next action)
```

Why split them? When a role closes, you mark the **role record** closed without losing your company research. When a new team opens a fresh requisition six months later, you create a new role record without overwriting the history of your earlier submission.

## 3. Fields to Track for Every Verified Opening

Store these eight fields in your private tracker — leave unconfirmed cells explicitly `Unknown` instead of guessing:

| Field | What to record |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Identity** | Employer, legal entity, exact role title, job or requisition ID, city or remote policy |
| **State** | `Open`, `Closed`, `Withdrawn`, `Uncertain`, or `Historical` |
| **Source and date** | Direct ATS Uniform Resource Locator (URL) plus exact date you confirmed the role was live |
| **Technical fit** | Key responsibilities matched to your real projects plus honest skill gaps |
| **Work authorization** | Confirmed sponsorship and EU Blue Card support or existing-authorization requirement |
| **Compensation** | Published range or written recruiter figure (currency, base versus equity, date) |
| **Interview loop** | Confirmed stages, time limits, take-home versus pairing, allowed tools |
| **Next action** | One concrete step (for example, ask recruiter about Blue Card, send referral note) plus due date |

## 4. Refresh Rules That Keep Your Database Trustworthy

- **Verify right before applying:** Re-open the official ATS link, confirm the requisition is still live, and check if requirements changed.
- **Close dead roles immediately:** If a posting disappears from the ATS, mark it `Closed` (or run `job_board_watch.py` in watch mode to detect `GONE` or `REOPEN` signals automatically).
- **Prioritize transparently:** Rank roles in your private notes by **hard-constraint fit (visa and location), technical overlap, and next concrete action** — never by vague online "best culture" rankings.
- **Research just enough for the next gate:** Do a 30-minute [company profile](80.07-company-profiles.md) before screening; go deep on one product workflow (as in [Langfuse prep](80.19-langfuse-interview-prep.md) or [company prep sheets](80.32-company-prep-sheets.md)) once an interview is scheduled.

## Summary

Use verified official ATS hubs and live discovery scripts instead of static company spreadsheets. Separate company research, dated role requisitions, and private application logs into three clean layers, leave unconfirmed fields visibly `Unknown`, and refresh every posting at the employer's ATS before investing preparation time.

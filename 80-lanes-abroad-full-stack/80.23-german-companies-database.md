# Maintain a German Role Research Database

A useful target database tracks openings with evidence. The previous static company tiers repeated unsourced salaries, stacks, visa promises, and interview scripts, including duplicate names and historical businesses. They are not a current hiring database. Keep the actual target records in the [private canonical workspace](personal/README.md).

## First Principles

Research describes an entity and role at a point in time. Because vacancies and hiring conditions change independently, a useful record needs the exact identity, dated sources and uncertainty rather than a permanent company-wide fit label.

## Store an opening, not a company stereotype

Two teams at the same company can use different stacks and hiring arrangements. Identify an opening by employer, employing entity, role identifier, location, and source URL. Record the source's date or your check date so another reader can judge freshness.

| Field | What to record |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Identity | Legal or recruiting entity, role title, role ID, location |
| State | Open, closed, withdrawn, uncertain, or historical |
| Source | Official role URL, check date, and supporting excerpt or note |
| Fit | Required work, supported relevant experience, gaps |
| Hiring constraint | Existing-authorization requirement or confirmed support for the actual opening |
| Compensation | Published range or recruiter statement, currency, base/total distinction and date |
| Process | Confirmed stages, time limits, assistance policy and owner |
| Next action | One unresolved question or application step |

Missing fields stay unknown. Do not fill a salary cell with a plausible-looking market band or infer visa support from a remote label.

## Keep company, role, and application records separate

```text
company identity
  └─ role opening and dated evidence
       └─ private application status and next action
```

The company record can hold official product and engineering sources. The role record holds the actual requirements. The application record holds personal communication and submitted artifacts. This separation prevents an old recruiter answer from silently becoming a permanent policy for every team.

Deduplicate using the actual role identifier and employing entity where available. Brand renames and subsidiaries need an explicit relationship, not an assumed merge. A company name is neither proof of an open role nor evidence of relocation support.

## Refresh before acting

Open the official vacancy before applying. Confirm the opening still exists, compare the requirements with the saved record, and resolve any material hiring constraint. Update evidence and dates when something changes; do not overwrite a historical submission so it appears to have used today's facts.

When a role closes, mark it closed and stop treating it as actionable. When a source disappears, mark the affected claim uncertain until another authoritative source supports it. A historical engineering blog remains useful context but does not prove today's team stack.

## Prioritize with visible reasons

Use supported role fit, actual hiring constraints, location preferences, available time, and the next useful action. Keep uncertainty visible beside the reason. A ranking is your planning decision, not an objective “best engineering culture” or “highest salary” label.

Research only enough to make the next decision. Before an interview, deepen one product workflow and one relevant technical boundary. The [company research guide](80.07-company-profiles.md) explains that work; [Langfuse preparation](80.19-langfuse-interview-prep.md) demonstrates a focused product approach.

## Summary

Treat each opening as a dated record with a source, state, known constraints, and next action. Keep personal applications canonical and preserve historical context rather than copying static company tiers between guides.

# Learn a Team's Working Norms & German Employment Basics

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Specialized-lightgrey)](../docs/topic-importance.md)

Preparing for a German or European tech role by memorizing cultural caricatures (*"never ask about salary,"* *"direct feedback means they hate your idea"*) creates unnecessary anxiety and miscommunication. Engineering teams in Berlin or Munich range from international seed startups to traditional enterprises — confirm the **actual team's norms** and understand **three statutory German employment rules** before signing your contract.

## First Principles

Nationality never tells you a specific team's working language, code-review style, or contract terms. Make working agreements explicit during interviews and onboarding, and separate **statutory legal floors** from **negotiable contractual benefits**.

## 1. Clarify Five Working Norms Before Joining

| Area | Questions to ask the hiring manager or team |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Communication** | Are specifications, pull request (PR) reviews, Slack, and product meetings all in English? What are asynchronous response expectations? |
| **Collaboration** | How are architecture disagreements resolved, and who holds final decision ownership? |
| **Delivery** | What does "done" include (tests, documentation, rollout, monitoring, on-call ownership)? |
| **Workload** | What are contracted weekly hours, on-call rotation frequency, and time-off and overtime rules? |
| **Onboarding** | What does a strong first 30 and 90 days look like, and how often does formal feedback happen? |

**Handle direct engineering feedback calmly:** If a reviewer says *"this design has risks,"* take it at face value rather than reading hidden hostility into it. Respond concisely: *"Good point — I hear the concern is retry behavior after a timeout. I'll trace that failure case and share a proposed contract."*

## 2. Keep Interview Communication Clear and Punctual

Lead with your conclusion, explain the constraint and trade-off, and state clearly when you do not know something (plus how you would verify it). Confirm interview format, time limit, and allowed tools in advance, test audio and screen share early, and message immediately if delayed. Use [Behavioral Interviews](80.04-behavioral-eu-style.md) for real stories and [Take-Home Playbook](80.01-take-home-playbook.md) for assignment execution.

## 3. Three German Employment Law Basics Versus Contractual Perks

Never confuse **statutory German legal floors** with **typical tech-company contract terms** (which are often more generous — always verify in your written contract):

| Topic | Statutory German legal baseline | What to check in your actual contract |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Annual vacation** | **20 paid days** for a standard 5-day week (law states 24 working days on a 6-day basis). ([Federal Vacation Act (BUrlG)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/burlg/BJNR000020963.html)) | Many German tech contracts offer 26–30 days; check carryover and expiry rules |
| **Paid sick leave** | Employer continues full pay for up to **6 weeks per illness** after a **4-week initial qualifying period** (recurrence rules apply). ([Wage Continuation Act (EntgFG)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/entgfg/BJNR106500994.html)) | Check when a doctor's note (*AU*) is required (day 1 versus day 3) and health-insurer coverage rules |
| **Probation and notice** | Agreed probation up to **6 months** carries a **2-week statutory notice period**; post-probation notice follows contract or statute. ([BGB §622](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__622.html)) | Probation is *not* a fixed-term contract — check post-probation notice (often 1–3 months to month-end!) |

Discretionary benefits (learning budget, Deutschland-Ticket subsidy, home-office stipend, equity or Virtual Stock Option Plan (VSOP)) are **not** automatic under German law — confirm each one in writing before accepting.

## 4. Win the First 30 Days Through Focused Onboarding

Pick one core production workflow and learn its real data boundaries and deployment pipeline before proposing broad rewrites. Align early with your manager on your first meaningful deliverable, surface blockers promptly, and write crisp docs whenever you uncover undocumented setup or architecture rules.

## Summary

Replace national stereotypes with direct questions about team norms, communication language, and delivery expectations. Handle candid technical feedback constructively, distinguish German statutory minimums (20-day vacation floor, 6-week sick-pay continuation after 4 weeks, 2-week probation notice) from negotiated contract terms, and confirm every perk in writing.

# Learn a Team's Working Norms

Prepare for the actual team rather than treating nationality as a hiring rubric. Clear answers, punctual communication, supported examples, and useful questions travel well across workplaces. Working language, feedback style, hours, and interview stages still need confirmation for the specific role.

## First Principles

A team’s expectations cannot be deduced from a country label. Working agreements and contract terms must therefore be established directly; a cultural generalization cannot substitute for an explicit requirement or applicable legal rule.

## Make expectations explicit

| Area | Confirm with the team |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Communication | Working language, documentation language, response expectations and escalation |
| Collaboration | Review process, disagreement handling, decision owner and remote overlap |
| Delivery | Definition of completion, testing, operational responsibility and handoff |
| Workload | Contract hours, on-call duty, overtime arrangements and time off |
| Onboarding | Initial goals, support, feedback cadence and evaluation criteria |

Do not interpret “interesting idea” as a coded rejection or “this has risks” as an instruction to abandon the design. Ask which risk matters, state your understanding, and address it. Cultural stereotypes make communication less precise when they replace the speaker's actual words.

## Answer the question in a useful order

Give the conclusion, explain the constraint, show your action, and state its supported outcome. If a technical question is ambiguous, clarify the invariant before designing. If you do not know a fact, explain what you would check and keep that uncertainty visible.

For an interview, confirm the format, duration, assignment limit, and allowed assistance. Test the call setup and arrive ready at the agreed time. If delayed, communicate promptly. There is no universal German six-round loop or automatic rejection rule for a particular answer style.

Use the [behavioral guide](80.04-behavioral-eu-style.md) for genuine examples and the [take-home guide](80.01-take-home-playbook.md) for assignment boundaries. Avoid repeating those banks here.

## Discuss the working conditions that affect your decision

Compensation, leave, on-call work, relocation, remote arrangements, and expected hours are legitimate topics to clarify. Choose the appropriate recruiter or hiring-stage conversation; do not assume that asking about salary early is forbidden.

Distinguish statutory entitlements, contractual terms, and discretionary benefits. A learning budget, transport allowance, equity grant, or home-office budget must be confirmed rather than inferred from a “German tech company” label.

The statutory annual-leave baseline is 24 working days under the law's six-day-week definition, equivalent to 20 days for a regular five-day week; actual accrual and contract terms matter. See the [Federal Vacation Act](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/burlg/BJNR000020963.html).

Employer wage continuation for illness is not unlimited paid leave from the first employment day: the statute sets conditions, generally a four-week qualifying employment period and up to six weeks for the relevant illness, with rules for recurrence. See the [Wage Continuation Act](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/entgfg/BJNR106500994.html).

An agreed probation period does not automatically mean a six-month fixed-term contract becomes permanent. The two-week statutory notice provision applies during agreed probation for at most six months, with relevant contractual or collective rules to consider. See [BGB §622](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__622.html).

## Use onboarding to reduce uncertainty

Agree on the first useful contribution, the person to ask for context, and how feedback will arrive. Learn one workflow and its operational boundary before proposing broad architectural change. Share blockers early and document decisions that others need to use.

A useful feedback response is: “I understand the concern is the retry behavior after a timeout. I will trace that case and bring back a proposed contract.” It states the issue and the next action without pretending that directness means agreement or that disagreement is disrespectful.

## Summary

Confirm the team's actual expectations, communicate concretely, and clarify the contract terms that affect your decision. National stereotypes and fixed interview scripts cannot replace that evidence.

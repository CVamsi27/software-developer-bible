# Choose Preparation from the Interview Format

Confirm the next interview's format and constraints, then prepare for its observed gaps. Use the [four-week study plan](80.13-study-plan.md) for a schedule. This guide selects the work; it does not add another competing calendar or promise an offer.

## First Principles

Study time is finite and interview formats assess different work. Preparation must therefore choose tasks from the confirmed format and observed gaps; reading every topic indiscriminately leaves less time to repair the relevant failure.

## Begin with the next decision

Record the role, stage, date, duration, permitted tools, and any required submission. If those details are unknown, ask the recruiter while continuing relevant fundamentals. Geography alone does not establish the interview loop.

| Confirmed stage | First preparation task | Useful guide |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Recruiter conversation | Explain supported experience and clarify material hiring constraints | [Company research](80.07-company-profiles.md) |
| Coding exercise | Try an unfamiliar variation and explain its invariant | [Coding patterns](80.02-tech-screen-patterns.md) |
| Project review | Trace one actual workflow with a few evidence cards | [Walkthrough](80.16-docita-resume-defense.md) |
| System design | Derive the domain invariant and trace a failure | [Design method](80.03-system-design-eu-patterns.md) |
| Behavioral interview | Select real events matching the competency | [Story-bank method](80.12-story-bank.md) |
| Take-home assignment | Follow the actual brief and timebox | [Take-home playbook](80.01-take-home-playbook.md) |
| Offer discussion | Compare actual cash, equity, conditions and relocation terms | [Compensation](80.05-comp-eu-benchmarking.md) |

## Use a short feedback loop

```text
attempt without notes → observe the gap → study the missing mechanism
  → try a changed case → record the next action
```

Reading hundreds of answers is not the same as explaining an unfamiliar case. Rehearse aloud or produce a small artifact, then compare it with the contract. Use [category revision](REVISION.md) for recall after learning and the [mock bank](80.11-mock-interview-bank.md) for a complete rehearsal.

A hesitation is not automatically a knowledge failure. Look for unsupported claims, a missing constraint, incorrect behavior, poor time allocation, or an explanation that obscures the decision. Practice the concrete weakness instead of trying to remove every pause.

## Allocate the time you actually have

Fit the next stage into the study plan's available budget. A real assignment replaces lower-priority practice. With only a week, prioritize the confirmed upcoming stage and the highest-risk gap; do not insist on a fresh portfolio project if the interview does not require one.

Keep actual applications and commitments in the [private workspace](personal/README.md). Avoid application-volume targets that make quality or role constraints disappear. A submitted application is a campaign action, not evidence that a technical gap has closed.

## Check readiness through behavior

A useful readiness signal is that you can explain the relevant invariant, handle a changed assumption, support the claims you use, and complete the agreed format within its time. Passing a mock shows performance in that mock; it cannot guarantee a hiring decision.

After a real interview, record the questions, observable difficulties, and any feedback you received. Keep inference separate from direct feedback. Change the next practice task when the evidence supports it.

## Summary

Use the next confirmed stage to choose preparation. Attempt, diagnose, study and retry inside one realistic budget. Readiness is observed behavior, not a count of memorized answers or a promise of offers.

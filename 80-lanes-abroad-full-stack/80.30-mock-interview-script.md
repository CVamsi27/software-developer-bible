# Run and Review a Mock Interview

A mock gives you a sample of performance under a chosen format. It cannot assign a senior/staff level from an unvalidated score or predict an offer. Select prompts from the [mock bank](80.11-mock-interview-bank.md); this guide covers facilitation and feedback.

## First Principles

A mock supplies useful feedback only when the observed attempt is separated from the reviewer’s assumptions. Its prompts, timing and debrief must therefore produce a specific repair action, rather than an invented employer pass score.

## Agree on the contract

Choose the target stage, duration, permitted notes/tools, recording consent, and feedback focus. Use a private solo recording when practicing alone. Do not record other people without their agreement or share sensitive project material.

One possible sixty-minute project/design mock is:

| Block | Minutes | Purpose |
| ------------------------------ | ------: | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Set the scope | 5 | Confirm format and constraints |
| Design or workflow explanation | 20 | Clarify the invariant and explain the mechanism |
| Changed-assumption follow-ups | 15 | Explore failure, recovery and alternatives |
| Genuine ownership example | 10 | Explain your action and supported result |
| Feedback and next action | 10 | Identify the most consequential gap |

The blocks total sixty minutes. Use a different allocation when the actual stage is coding or behavioral. Do not label ten six-minute questions plus a ten-minute question as the same sixty-minute session.

## Probe the mechanism

Use follow-ups to change an assumption: a second request arrives, a worker loses ownership, a provider accepts before a crash, a client reconnects, or a permission changes. Give the candidate time to reason. Artificial stress is not automatically useful or representative of the real loop.

Keep hypothetical proposals distinct from current project claims. An outbox and CDC are not necessarily alternatives: CDC can relay outbox rows. MVCC reduces read/write conflicts but does not eliminate every lock. Sensitive-data caching depends on authorization, purpose, storage and freshness boundaries rather than a blanket label.

For a concurrency claim, establish the exact capacity and request identities before expecting one success and the rest conflicts. A mock explanation does not prove the implementation works; identify the database test or other evidence that would establish it.

## Write feedback as observations

| Dimension | Observation to record |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Correctness | Specific invariant, trace or assumption that was right or wrong |
| Evidence | Which implementation/result claim was supported or overbroad |
| Tradeoff | Whether the alternative and its consequence were explained |
| Communication | Where the listener could or could not follow the mechanism |
| Time | Which required part was omitted or crowded out |

Separate the observed behavior from your interpretation. “Did not explain uncertain provider acceptance” is actionable; “not staff-level” is not a diagnosis. Repeating the exact prompt can show rehearsal effects, so add a changed case before attributing improvement to transferable skill.

## Choose one repair and recheck

After feedback, select the highest-risk gap. Read the canonical lesson, produce a corrected explanation or implementation, then rehearse a related variation. Record the attempt and next recheck in private progress records. Use the [study plan](80.13-study-plan.md) for the time budget rather than adding another daily schedule.

For behavioral answers, use events that actually happened and distinguish your contribution from the team's. A suggested production mistake is not permission to claim that incident as your own.

## Summary

Agree on a format, probe a changed assumption, record concrete observations, and fix one consequential gap. Use the result to guide preparation, not to manufacture a hiring-level score.

# Discuss and Negotiate an Actual Offer

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Specialized-lightgrey)](../docs/topic-importance.md)



Use the [compensation guide](80.05-comp-eu-benchmarking.md) to compare an offer. This guide covers the conversation and the written agreement. There is no universal German rule to counter by ten or twenty percent, reject the first offer, or treat negotiation as guaranteed.

## First Principles

An offer contains several terms with different value and conditions. A supported counterproposal must identify the priority it improves and the evidence behind the request; the first offered number does not imply a universal hidden percentage to recover.

## Clarify the offer before making a counter

Confirm the employing entity, role level, base currency and payment schedule, variable-pay conditions, equity terms, location, expected hours, start-date conditions, and decision deadline. Keep actual offers and your preferences in the [private canonical records](personal/README.md).

Separate recurring guaranteed cash, one-time payments, contingent bonus, and uncertain equity. A signing payment can bridge first-year cash but does not improve recurring base. An option percentage without the grant's terms, dilution context, exercise costs and liquidity is not a cash-equivalent value.

## Choose the term that matters most

| Concern | Specific discussion |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recurring income | A supported base target and the role's approved band |
| Upfront move cost | Covered items, caps, payment timing, tax treatment and repayment terms |
| Work location | Written remote/hybrid arrangement and permitted jurisdictions |
| Equity uncertainty | Grant instrument, vesting, leaver terms, exercise and liquidity |
| Time and workload | Leave, contractual hours, on-call duty and overtime arrangements |
| Delayed admission | Start-date contingency and documentation owner |

Prioritize according to your actual constraints. Equity is not always the second-most valuable term, and an extra monitor is not equivalent to predictable recurring income.

## Make a concise supported request

A neutral shape is: “Thank you for the offer. The role is attractive because of [actual reason]. Based on [relevant evidence or constraint], my priority is [specific term]. Is there room to adjust it to [supported request]?”

Adapt the wording to facts you can defend. Do not invent another offer, a previous leave entitlement, remote experience, or a comparable relocation package. Asking about compensation in an early recruiter conversation can save time when expectations differ materially.

If the term is fixed, clarify whether another term can address the actual concern. Avoid assuming every employer has hidden negotiation room or promising a particular settlement. Accepting an acceptable first offer can be a reasonable decision.

## Check admission separately from market value

A visa threshold is an eligibility condition, not your market-value anchor. Use the current [visa guide](80.06-visa-relocation-playbook.md) and actual route requirements. Being above a threshold does not by itself prove that every qualification, offer, approval and admission condition is met.

Recheck age-dependent criteria and salary tables for the specific route and date. Do not predict a six-week process from a company's past experience. Confirm responsibilities and timing contingencies through the [employer-support guide](80.20-germany-visa-relocation-companies.md).

## Preserve the agreement in writing

Choose a communication channel both parties can use effectively; email can help keep complex terms precise. Request the time you need to review within the actual deadline. Summarize material changes and confirm they appear in the written offer, contract, or appropriate grant document before relying on them.

Check notice, probation, restrictive covenants, variable-pay conditions, remote terms and repayment clauses in their actual documents. Use the notice terms applicable to the actual probation arrangement; [working terms](80.25-german-workplace-culture.md) distinguishes the statutory framework from the contract.

## Summary

Understand the actual package, choose the most consequential term, make a supported request, and confirm the resulting agreement in writing. Keep immigration eligibility and uncertain equity separate from recurring compensation.

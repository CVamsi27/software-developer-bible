# Company Product Preparation Sheets

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)

Walking into a product-company interview saying *"I've used React and NestJS"* sounds interchangeable with twenty other candidates. Walking in able to trace **one real user workflow in that company's product** — and connect it honestly to a mechanism you engineered — immediately signals senior product judgment.

The six reference sheets below cover real European product archetypes (official product pages checked on `2026-10-01`). They teach you **how to study a product's core engineering workflow**; always verify the live opening's stack, team, salary, and visa policy directly on the employer's Applicant Tracking System (ATS).

## First Principles

A public product page shows what the customer pays for and where failures hurt most — it does **not** reveal a private team's internal microservices or exact interview script. Good preparation therefore separates **what you verified from public product documentation**, **which engineering mechanism from your own past work transfers**, and **which domain specifics you need to ask the team about**.

## 1. Build a 1-Page Product Prep Sheet Before Any Technical Round

| Section | What to write down (5–10 bullets max) |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Role and entity** | Live ATS URL, exact job title and ID, city or remote scope, and date checked |
| **Core workflow** | One customer action from input to output — and what breaks if it fails |
| **Transferable proof** | One real system decision you owned that solves a similar failure mode |
| **Honest gap** | One domain or stack piece you have *not* used in production yet |
| **Smart question** | One architectural trade-off question to ask the hiring manager |

Never fabricate domain experience (like claiming clinical Medical Device Regulation (MDR) certification or satellite machine learning (ML) experience if you haven't shipped it). Saying *"I haven't built `PostGIS` pipelines yet, but here's how I debugged high-fanout spatial and index queries in PostgreSQL"* builds far more credibility.

## 2. Six Reference Product Workflows

### LiveEO — Satellite Imagery to Operational Risk Alerts

LiveEO delivers satellite-driven vegetation, pipeline, and deforestation risk monitoring ([product overview](https://www.live-eo.com/)).
- **Workflow to trace:** `satellite observation ─► geospatial processing ─► risk alert ─► operator action`.
- **Engineering angles worth studying:** data provenance, acquisition timestamp versus delivery latency, spatial query indexing, and showing uncertainty clearly in the user interface (UI).
- **Study companion:** [Geospatial & satellite primer](80.34-germany-target-domain-primer.md#part-ii--geospatial--satellite-analytics-liveeo). Confirm the specific team's frontend and backend split rather than assuming every role touches machine learning (ML) pipelines.

### Snke — Clinical Information Across Healthcare Systems

Snke builds an orchestrated healthcare technology ecosystem ([product site](https://www.snke.com/)).
- **Workflow to trace:** `source clinical system ─► patient identity & consent boundary ─► normalized data ─► authorized clinical view`.
- **Engineering angles worth studying:** multi-system interoperability, strict tenant and patient identity isolation, audit logs, and unambiguous units and timestamps.
- **Study companion:** [Healthtech Germany primer](80.34-germany-target-domain-primer.md#part-i--healthtech-germany-snke--mvst--redcare). Distinguish standard Software as a Service (SaaS) multi-tenancy from regulated medical-device workflows.

### MVST — Client Brief to Shipped, Maintainable Product

MVST runs product design and software delivery engagements ([official site](https://www.mvst.co/)).
- **Workflow to trace:** `client brief ─► scoped milestone ─► build & review ─► clean handoff for ongoing change`.
- **Engineering angles worth studying:** negotiating scope trade-offs under fixed timelines, writing maintainable codebases for handoff, and explaining architecture clearly to non-technical stakeholders.
- **Study companion:** [Take-Home Masterclass](80.26-take-home-masterclass.md) for how to present a clean, well-documented repository.

### Redcare Pharmacy — Regulated Online Pharmacy Orders

Redcare operates digital pharmacy services across European markets ([company site](https://www.redcare-pharmacy.com/home)).
- **Workflow to trace:** `authenticated order ─► prescription validation ─► fulfillment state machine ─► customer notifications`.
- **Engineering angles worth studying:** order state transitions, idempotency across external prescription and payment gateways, privacy boundaries, and country-specific rules across European Union (EU) markets.
- **Study companion:** [German System Design Patterns](80.27-german-system-design-patterns.md) for handling uncertain external dependencies cleanly.

### Tanso — Raw Industrial Inputs to Auditable ESG Reports

Tanso builds carbon accounting and Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) management software for industrial companies ([product site](https://www.tanso.de/)).
- **Workflow to trace:** `raw activity data ─► unit & emission-factor version ─► calculation ─► auditable report`.
- **Engineering angles worth studying:** versioned calculation formulas, immutable audit trails, and safely recomputing historical totals when an input or factor changes.
- **Study companion:** [EU Regulatory Compliance](80.24-eu-regulatory-compliance.md) to keep compliance scope accurate.

### n8n — Workflow Automation Across Flaky External APIs

n8n builds a visual and code-extensible workflow automation platform ([product site](https://n8n.io/)).
- **Workflow to trace:** `trigger ─► versioned workflow execution ─► external API step ─► partial failure, retry & recovery`.
- **Engineering angles worth studying:** distinguishing safe retries from duplicate external side effects (idempotency keys), durable execution state, webhook backpressure, and clear operator error visibility across external application programming interfaces (APIs).
- **Study companion:** [Take-Home Starter Prompt](80.15-take-home-starter-prompt.md) for practicing a clean, scoped workflow build.

## Summary

Pick one real customer workflow for each company you interview with, trace where data or reliability can fail along that path, pair it with one honest transferable story from your own production work, and keep unverified team details as questions for the interviewer.

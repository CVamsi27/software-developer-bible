## First Principles

A company’s product creates a useful discussion context, but does not reveal a team’s current architecture or interview loop. Preparation must separate verified product behavior from a hypothetical design and clarify the actual role with its owner.

## Build a short role sheet

| Field | Keep it specific |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Role | Official opening, employing entity, location and check date |
| Product workflow | One user action and the consequence of failure |
| Fit | One supported prior decision, plus a clearly named experience gap |
| Preparation | One source path, mechanism or domain lesson to study |
| Unknown | The most important unresolved role or process detail |

Do not memorize a first-person pitch that claims technology or incidents you have not used. A transferable mechanism is useful when its difference from the new domain is explicit.

## LiveEO: imagery to an operational decision

LiveEO describes satellite-based vegetation, pipeline and deforestation risk products. The preparation path is **observation → processing → risk result → user action**. Inspect provenance, acquisition time, uncertainty, spatial query cost and how a result reaches a user. This is a suggested study angle rather than a claimed interview question. [Official product overview](https://www.live-eo.com/).

Use the [geospatial primer](80.34-germany-target-domain-primer.md#part-ii--geospatial--satellite-analytics-liveeo) to learn the data distinctions. Prior database work can demonstrate query reasoning; it does not establish professional PostGIS or satellite-model experience. Confirm the actual team's browser/backend split and role requirements.

## Snke: clinical information across systems

Snke presents an orchestrated healthtech ecosystem. Study **source system → identity and consent boundary → normalized information → authorized clinical use**. Interoperability, intended use, clinical context and interface ownership matter more than repeating a framework list. [Official product source](https://www.snke.com/).

A clinic application's tenant boundary can support a comparison, but does not establish operating-room device expertise or medical-device release experience. Use the [healthtech primer](80.34-germany-target-domain-primer.md#part-i--healthtech-germany-snke--mvst--redcare) and confirm the actual product's assessed regulatory scope.

## MVST: client brief to maintained product

MVST describes product design and development services. Study **client need → bounded scope → delivery decision → handoff and change**. Prepare a genuine example of negotiating a requirement and explaining its consequence. [Official service source](https://www.mvst.co/).

Clarify the role's client responsibilities, project allocation and technologies. End-to-end web ownership does not automatically establish React Native, every cloud provider, or production AI expertise. Use the [handoff review](80.26-take-home-masterclass.md) for a concrete artifact explanation.

## Redcare Pharmacy: an order through a healthcare service

Redcare describes online pharmacy services across European markets. Study **authorized order → applicable prescription workflow → fulfillment state → user communication**. Separate correctness, privacy, operational tracking and market-specific requirements. [Official company source](https://www.redcare-pharmacy.com/home).

The study angle does not prove the team's current languages, location or architecture. Use the [domain constraints](80.27-german-system-design-patterns.md) to reason about uncertain external outcomes and verify the role-specific implementation before naming technologies.

## Tanso: input evidence to a reported calculation

Tanso describes carbon accounting and ESG-management software for industrial businesses. Study **source data → units and methodology version → calculation → reviewable result**. A corrected source or changed factor should have an explicit recomputation and provenance story. [Official product source](https://www.tanso.de/).

Auditability transfers as a mechanism; the domain's reporting standards and applicability need separate study. Use the [compliance guide](80.24-eu-regulatory-compliance.md) to distinguish scope from a product label, and check the actual opening's stack.

## n8n: a workflow across failure boundaries

n8n describes a workflow automation platform. Study **trigger → versioned execution → external step → failure and recovery**. Distinguish retrying a step from safely repeating its remote effect. [Official product source](https://n8n.io/).

Do not assume the vacancy requires a workflow take-home, a particular workweek, or relocation support. Use the [workflow starter](80.15-take-home-starter-prompt.md) as a bounded practice exercise, then confirm the actual loop.

## Summary

Choose one product workflow and one supported comparison. Verify the actual role's requirements separately and leave experience gaps explicit.

## 1. LiveEO (live-eo.com) — satellite-powered risk intelligence 🎯 TOP TARGET

### Company Profile (verified against live postings)

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Industry** | Earth observation / satellite analytics / supply-chain compliance |
| **Products** | Treeline (grid vegetation risk) · SurfaceScout (pipeline threats) · TradeAware (**EUDR** deforestation compliance) · Sektion 4 (government R&D) · **Twinspector** (own VHR stereo satellite constellation!) |
| **Size / HQ** | ~130–160 people, Berlin Kreuzberg (hybrid), founded 2018 |
| **Funding** | Well-funded (Series A+); €6.6M (2026) for Twinspector constellation; SPRIND federal contract for optical↔SAR agentic-AI fusion |
| **Full-stack stack** | **React, TypeScript, NestJS, TypeORM, Python**, PostgreSQL incl. geospatial workloads, GitLab CI; infra per LinkedIn: Terraform, K8s/EKS, ArgoCD, Helm, Auth0, Coralogix |
| **ML stack** | PyTorch/Lightning, MLflow, Databricks, Ray, Prefect, GDAL/Rasterio/GeoPandas/STAC |

### What They Care About (from actual job ads)

1. **Geospatial performance** — "rendering and querying large volumes of geospatial data efficiently in browser AND backend"; fast DB queries are called out explicitly
2. **End-to-end ownership** — small product team (~5 engineers), features owned design→production
3. **AI-assisted engineering** — they now *require* candidates who use AI coding tools daily ("in your actual day-to-day, not just tried once")
4. **Spec-driven / TDD culture** — mention your test discipline unprompted
5. **Third-party APIs** — integrations for external consumers of their services

### Interview Process (their careers page)

1. Apply online → 2. Hiring manager + HR screen → 3. Team interviews → 4. **Business-case challenge** (take-home style, geospatial-flavored) → 5. Final with senior management

### Likely Interview Questions

| Question | What They're Testing | Your prep |
|----------|---------------------|-----------|
| "Design an API serving vegetation-risk over 100K km of railway" | Spatial indexing, tiling, caching | [Domain primer §Geospatial](80.34-germany-target-domain-primer.md#geospatial-liveeo) |
| "How would you render huge geometries in the browser without freezing it?" | Vector tiles, simplification, WebGL | primer: tile pyramids, ST_AsMVT |
| "Design a change-detection alerting pipeline from imagery ingest to notification" | Event-driven, queues, idempotency | **Your outbox/DLQ story maps directly** |
| "Multi-tenant geospatial data — how?" | Tenant scoping + spatial partitioning | Docita ALS tenancy + row scoping story |
| "How do you keep map queries fast as data grows?" | GiST indexes, partitioning, EXPLAIN | Your Postgres performance material |

### Talking Points (rehearse aloud)

- **Stack overlap is real:** "NestJS + TypeScript + PostgreSQL is my production stack at Docita — including the performance work: execution plans, composite/GiST-style indexing strategy, connection pooling."
- **Event pipelines:** "Imagery ingest → detection → alert is a queue/outbox problem. I built a transactional outbox + DLQ with replay in production healthcare — identical failure-mode thinking."
- **AI-tooling fluency:** "I use AI assistants daily for scaffolding and review acceleration — and I gate generated code behind CI invariant checks, which is also how I'd adopt it responsibly here." *(they ask this)*
- **Domain bridge:** "Healthcare taught me sensitive-data discipline (RLS, audit trails). EUDR compliance and infrastructure monitoring have the same shape: provenance, auditability, regulator-facing exports."

### Questions to Ask Them

1. "How do you split geospatial work between PostGIS, Python services, and the browser today?"
2. "What does the business-case challenge look like — product-scope or algorithm-depth?"
3. "With Twinspector coming, how will the product stack evolve around STAC/COG-native data?"

## 2. Snke (snke.com, formerly Snke OS) — hospital IT & surgical AI platform 🎯 TOP TARGET

### Company Profile (verified)

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Industry** | Healthtech: digital OR, hospital data infrastructure, medical AI/XR |
| **Origin** | Spun off from **Brainlab** (June 2025); Brainlab tech = digital ecosystem in ~4,000 hospitals/120 countries, €500M+ invested over 30 years |
| **Size / HQ** | ~375+ experts; Munich Bogenhausen HQ; offices Chicago, Heidelberg, Tel Aviv |
| **Traction** | ~150 clinics run Snke solutions; ~20K patient cases/year |
| **Portfolio** | Digital-OR edge devices · clinical data repositories · AI anatomical modeling · structured radiology reporting · immersive visualization/simulation · **HX Now** (mobile patient records integrated with i.s.h.med) · **SnkeXR** open AR headset for medicine (2026) |
| **Positioning** | "Trusted orchestration layer" between medical devices and hospital IT; open interfaces for third-party medtech |
| **Investor** | EMH Partners (2025) |

### What They Care About

1. **Platform + integration mindset** — open APIs, vendor-neutral EHR strategy, third-party device integration (HL7/DICOM world)
2. **Regulated software discipline** — medical device software means MDR process, traceability, verification rigor
3. **Data gravity done right** — clinical repositories, patient models, privacy by architecture
4. **Real-time, edge-adjacent systems** — OR integration is latency- and safety-sensitive

### Likely Interview Questions

| Question | What They're Testing | Your prep |
|----------|---------------------|-----------|
| "Design a hospital data platform integrating 50 heterogeneous systems" | FHIR/HL7, canonical models, sync patterns | [Primer §Healthtech](80.34-germany-target-domain-primer.md#healthtech-germany-snke-mvst-redcare) |
| "How do you guarantee PHI isolation across a multi-clinic platform?" | Tenancy, RLS, audit | **Your strongest story — Docita is exactly this** |
| "Surgeon needs live device data in the OR with <100ms latency" | Edge processing, local-first, graceful degradation | Edge/on-prem framing from primer |
| "How would you version APIs consumed by third-party medtech vendors?" | Contract testing, deprecation, backward compat | REST versioning files + webhook contract habits |
| "Walk us through making AI features compliant in clinical software" | MDR awareness, human-in-the-loop, logging | Primer MDR section + honest boundaries |

### Talking Points

- **Direct domain hit:** "I built and shipped a multi-tenant clinic platform — scheduling through prescriptions to billing — with tenant isolation enforced twice (app-layer context + database RLS), append-only audit trails, and PHI-safe observability. That's your orchestration-layer problem at smaller scale."
- **Integration empathy:** "I've integrated ABDM health-grid flows (consent artifacts, care contexts) and payment/messaging webhooks — heterogeneous external-system integration with idempotency and replay is my day job."
- **Compliance honesty:** "I know the difference between 'designed for GDPR/MDR expectations' and 'certified' — I can speak to controls, not certificates."

### Questions to Ask Them

1. "Where does the platform boundary sit between Brainlab-originated software and third-party devices — how do teams own interfaces?"
2. "How much of the stack is edge/on-prem vs cloud across those 150 clinics?"
3. "What does the path look like for software changes under your QMS — cycle time from PR to clinical release?"

## 3. MVST (mvst.co) — digital product studio ⚠️ CORRECTED PROFILE

> The old sheet described MVST as a Shopify-scaleup. Reality: **MVST is a digital product studio** — client work end-to-end (strategy → UX/UI → build → scale) across e-commerce, healthcare, energy, finance. Adjust your pitch accordingly: breadth, product sense, and client communication matter more than single-domain depth.

### Company Profile (verified)

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Industry** | Digital product studio / consultancy (web, mobile, AI products) |
| **Size / HQ** | 60+ people; Munich HQ (Schwabing) + Barcelona tech hub + remote (incl. Lisbon/Porto) |
| **Track record** | 100+ product launches, clients in DE/CH/AT/ES, 15+ industries |
| **Capabilities seen publicly** | Web platforms, React Native mobile, headless e-commerce, Storyblok CMS, cloud architectures (AWS/Azure), AI integrations (OpenAI, Azure ML, Lambda pipelines), PostgreSQL + Python analytics dashboards |
| **Culture** | Ownership-heavy, hybrid Munich, English-friendly, startup-speed |

### What They Care About

1. **Ship-versatility** — one senior full-stack covers frontend, backend, mobile touchpoints, and deployment
2. **Product judgment** — you'll face clients; UX sensitivity and scope-negotiation are interview topics
3. **AI-integration experience** — they market AI features heavily (OpenAI/Azure ML case studies)
4. **Consulting hygiene** — estimation, stakeholder communication, documentation

### Likely Interview Questions

| Question | What They're Testing |
|----------|---------------------|
| "Take a client brief: build a patient-engagement app in 12 weeks — how do you scope v1?" | Product scoping, MVP slicing |
| "When would you choose React Native over native or PWA?" | Mobile trade-offs |
| "You inherit a project with no tests and angry stakeholders — first 2 weeks?" | Triage, communication, delivery |
| "Show us something you built end-to-end" | Portfolio walkthrough (bring Docita!) |
| "How do you integrate LLM features safely into client products?" | Kill-switches, cost guards, evals — cite your AI-gating pattern |

### Talking Points

- "I'm used to being the engineer who owns slices end-to-end — at Docita I was founding engineer on a full clinic OS. Studio life suits me: varied domains, high ownership, direct stakeholder contact."
- "I can cover the full surface you sell: React/Next frontends, NestJS backends, React Native if needed, AWS deploy, and AI features with production guardrails."
- "Healthcare projects are where I'm rare among studio seniors: I've handled PHI-grade requirements — that's directly sellable to your health clients."

### Questions to Ask Them

1. "How do teams form around clients — pod model or matrix? Who owns architecture decisions?"
2. "What's the ratio of greenfield vs inherited-code work?"
3. "How does the Barcelona/Munich split work day-to-day?"

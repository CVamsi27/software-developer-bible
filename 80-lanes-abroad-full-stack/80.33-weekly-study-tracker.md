## Readiness Checklist

Before your first interview, you should be able to:

- [ ] Deliver 60-second pitch without notes
- [ ] Answer 8 STAR stories in under 2 minutes each
- [ ] Design a system in 45 minutes (whiteboard/paper)
- [ ] Explain MVCC, WAL, VACUUM without looking
- [ ] Explain outbox pattern, circuit breaker, saga
- [ ] Explain RBAC/ABAC, OAuth 2.0, JWT refresh
- [ ] Explain RED/USE monitoring, SLI/SLO/SLA
- [ ] Explain rate limiting algorithms (token bucket, sliding window)
- [ ] Score 40+/50 on mock interview
- [ ] Have "Why this company?" ready for 5 targets
- [ ] Have salary range in mind (€80K-€120K)
- [ ] Know your Blue Card eligibility

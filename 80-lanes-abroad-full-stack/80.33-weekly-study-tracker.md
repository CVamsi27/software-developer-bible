# Weekly Study Review Template

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)

Every week of interview prep drifts unless you spend 15 minutes comparing **what you planned** against **what actually worked**. Your [Four-Week Study Plan](80.13-study-plan.md) holds the schedule and your [Weekly Tracker](80.10-weekly-tracker.md) logs individual gap rows; copy this clean 8-field review template into your private workspace every Sunday to steer the upcoming week cleanly.

## First Principles

Checking off reading boxes measures *time spent*, not *interview skill gained*. Keeping planned hours, actual hours, and real changed-case outcomes visible side-by-side forces honest replanning instead of silent backlog pileups.

## 1. The 8-Field Sunday Review Template (Copy Into Your Private Log)

| Field | What to fill in (2–3 lines max) |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Week and real hour budget** | Exact dates plus realistic hours available this week |
| **Next confirmed interview stage** | Upcoming format, date, and required preparation (if any) |
| **Primary learning goal** | One concrete mechanism or explanation to strengthen |
| **Planned blocks** | Specific chapters, variations, or mocks scheduled |
| **Actual hours spent** | Honest total including setup, debugging, and debrief |
| **What broke or worked** | Exact invariant nailed, missed assumption, or blocked test |
| **Concrete proof** | Link to private mock notes, test run, or clean checkout |
| **Next week's #1 action** | One targeted repair plus delayed changed-case recheck |

Never hide unfinished work behind a vague confidence score — leave incomplete or blocked tasks visible so you fix the bottleneck.

## 2. How to Adjust Next Week From Six Common Signals

| What happened this week | Exact adjustment for next week |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A real take-home or interview landed** | Swap out lower-priority practice blocks; keep total weekly hours sane |
| **Environment setup ate half a session** | Shrink optional scope and fix clean-checkout scripts first |
| **Same mock prompt felt easier on retry** | Test an *unseen* constraint variation to confirm real transfer |
| **One failure boundary stayed fuzzy** | Read that single canonical chapter and draw the crash trace cold |
| **Target company confirmed a different loop** | Re-rank preparation immediately via [Interview-Format Roadmap](80.28-interview-prep-roadmap.md) |
| **Missed multiple sessions due to work or fatigue** | Trim the weekly target instead of rolling every unfinished task forward |

Keep **learning progress** and **job-application counts** cleanly separated — sending ten applications is pipeline work, not proof that a system-design gap is closed.

## Summary

Run a 15-minute Sunday review comparing planned budget, actual hours, and observed changed-case outcomes in your private workspace. Carry forward only the highest-risk unresolved gap, drop redundant reading, and adjust next week's budget realistically.

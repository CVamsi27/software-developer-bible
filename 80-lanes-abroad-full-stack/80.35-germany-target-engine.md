# Germany Target Engine — Turn Live Vacancies Into High-Fit Applications

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Specialized-lightgrey)](../docs/topic-importance.md)

> **Purpose:** Run a calm, repeatable weekly system that turns verified live openings into targeted senior applications. Keep your actual target list, CVs, recruiter contacts, and statuses strictly in your private workspace.
>
> Companions: [Find Startup Roles](../00-strategy/00.05-find-startup-roles.md) · [Company Prep Sheets](80.32-company-prep-sheets.md) · [Domain Primer](80.34-germany-target-domain-primer.md) · [Comp Benchmarking](80.05-comp-eu-benchmarking.md) · [Visa Playbook](80.06-visa-relocation-playbook.md)

## First Principles

Job searches fail in two opposite ways: either **spray-and-pray** (sending 100 generic applications to stale postings and getting ghosted) or **over-researching** (spending three days reading about one company before checking whether they even hire non-EU candidates). A healthy target engine runs a tight weekly loop: **verify live ATS opening → screen hard constraints in 5 minutes → tailor truthfully → track real outcomes**.

## 1. Always Start With the Live Requisition, Never a Brand Name

Even a well-known German tech company can have one team hiring English-speaking full-stack engineers with European Union (EU) Blue Card support and another team requiring fluent C1 German and existing residency. Before tailoring anything:
1. Open the employer's live Applicant Tracking System (ATS) Uniform Resource Locator (URL) (discovered via venture capital (VC) boards, direct ATS queries, or `scripts/job_board_watch.py --discover`).
2. Record the exact **URL, job ID, location and remote policy, core stack, and today's date** in your private tracker.
3. If an older link is `404` or marked filled, mark it `Closed` immediately — never assume an old bookmark is still hiring.

## 2. Screen Every Role With a 5-Minute Reality Check

Fill three columns honestly before spending time customizing your curriculum vitae (CV):

| Gate question | Verified fact from the posting | Open question to confirm in Screen #1 |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Location and remote** | Exact city, hybrid days, or permitted remote countries | Whether relocation or interim remote work is allowed |
| **Technical ownership** | Core responsibilities matched to real projects you built | Any unfamiliar domain or stack tool and onboarding expectations |
| **Working language** | Explicit English versus German requirement in the job description (JD) | Whether daily team specifications, pull requests (PRs), and product meetings are in English |
| **Visa and relocation** | Stated relocation support or EU-only restriction | Written employer support for EU Blue Card paperwork and start-date flexibility |
| **Worth applying now?** | Live opening, strong overlap, and clear next step | Actual interview stages and budgeted salary band |

Never assume `"English-speaking office"` means `"sponsors relocation,"` and never confuse a qualifying degree with complete visa readiness — verify route conditions in the [Visa Playbook](80.06-visa-relocation-playbook.md).

## 3. Keep Four Clean Records in Your Private Workspace

Avoid spreading notes across random documentation files, spreadsheets, and chat logs. Maintain four distinct files in your private workspace keyed by **company + role ID**:

1. **Targets:** Shortlisted companies, official ATS board links, and why each fits.
2. **Application tracker:** Role ID, live URL, date applied, exact CV version submitted, current stage, and next follow-up date.
3. **Outreach log:** Referral or hiring-manager messages sent and promised reply dates.
4. **Master CV:** Your complete, verified project evidence from which every tailored resume is drawn.

## 4. Use Local Automation Cleanly Without Losing Control

If you use local scripts like [`scripts/job_board_watch.py`](../docs/scripts-map.md) or an external local workspace tool like `career-ops`:
- Keep all personal data (CVs, target lists, generated Portable Document Format (PDF) files, tracker rows) inside your **private workspace repository**, never in this public study repository and never committed to an upstream tool fork.
- Run your private workspace's symlink and health check after generating new artifacts.
- **Inspect every generated resume, cover letter, and fit score before submitting:** automated scoring cannot verify visa eligibility, prove a technical claim, or catch an invented bullet.

## 5. Diagnose Rejections Without Guessing

A fast automated rejection rarely explains why you were filtered out — it could be location or visa, timing (role nearly filled), internal transfer, or an unclear resume bullet.

- **If the posting clearly had a hard mismatch** (e.g., required German residency), log that filter and tighten your discovery queries.
- **If the role genuinely matched your background,** check whether your top 3 resume bullets surfaced the exact system problems the JD emphasized, and ask politely for feedback if you reached a human screen.
- **Keep the exact submitted CV version** linked in your tracker so you can see what actually converted over time.

## 6. Run a 20-Minute Weekly Funnel Review

Every Sunday, count real observed numbers with explicit denominators — e.g., *"10 verified applications submitted in November → 3 recruiter screens → 2 technical loops"* — instead of obsessing over daily silence. Pick your top 3 concrete actions for the coming week: run fresh ATS discovery, send 2 targeted referral messages, close stale applications, and deepen one technical gap exposed in recent interviews.

## Summary

Drive your job search from verified live ATS requisitions rather than company stereotypes. Run a fast 5-minute constraint screen before tailoring, keep targets, applications, outreach, and CVs cleanly separated in your private workspace, review every automated output before submission, and steer each week from real funnel conversion data.

# System Design, APIs & Security (Phases 16–19)

---

## First Principles

1. **Design rounds for SDE-2 in India probe *trade-off articulation* more than exotic scale**: the bar is can you state what you'd sacrifice (consistency vs latency, cost vs redundancy) — the API/security material exists because those are the most common follow-up probes.
2. **Security questions are checklist-predictable**: authN vs authZ, OWASP top items, secrets handling — high ROI per study hour because the question set is small and stable across loops (→ 70.3's decision-index principle).

## Phase 16: System Design

> **Why It Matters:** System design is the most important interview round for SDE II+ roles. It tests your ability to architect scalable, reliable, and maintainable systems. You'll typically have 45 minutes to design a complex system from scratch.

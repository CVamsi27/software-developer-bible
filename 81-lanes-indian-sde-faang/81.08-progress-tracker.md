# Progress Tracker — Turn an Attempt Into the Next Action

[![Role priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/role--priority-Deferred-lightgrey)](../docs/topic-importance.md)
[![General importance](https://img.shields.io/badge/general--importance-Important-blue)](../docs/topic-importance.md)



## Record the task before scoring yourself

A useful record tells you what you tried, what happened and what needs repair. “Read graphs” or “confidence eight out of ten” does not show whether you can detect a cycle or explain a shortest-path condition.

Use this blank template for your own preparation. Keep personal records in the [canonical personal workspace](../80-lanes-abroad-full-stack/personal/README.md), rather than filling private experience into a shared learning guide.

## First Principles

Reading, recognizing, reproducing and applying a mechanism are different observations. An attempt can be correct with a hint or incomplete without one. Tracking must preserve those distinctions so the next plan responds to an actual gap instead of an exposure count or invented readiness score.

## Keep one attempt log

| Date | Task and source | Assistance | Observed result | Gap | Next action and due date |
| ----------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Actual date | Exact prompt or chapter | None, hint, source or tool | What ran or was explained | Specific missing mechanism or case | Repair and later check |

For example, record “binary-search implementation passed the middle case but missed an empty array; used a hint to define the interval.” The next action is to repair that boundary and try a changed input later. Do not record “binary search mastered” from one familiar solution.

A task’s result should include its relevant checks: edge cases and complexity for coding; invariant and failure boundary for design; supported contribution and outcome for an experience story. Keep subjective confidence separate from that evidence.

## Maintain a short gap queue

| Gap | Relevant assessment | Canonical source | Repair task | Evidence from later attempt |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ---------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Specific mechanism | Confirmed format or role requirement | Link | Small observable task | What changed; assistance used |

Select the next session from this queue and the [coding study sequence](81.06-leetcode-study-plan.md). If a repair is deferred, retain its reason rather than marking it complete. If the role changes, reprioritize the queue.

## Log mocks by the observed failure

Use [the mock bank](81.09-mock-interview-question-bank.md) to select prompts. After the attempt, separate the reviewer’s observation from the suggested repair.

| Mock date and format | Observation | Evidence | Repair | Recheck |
| -------------------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Actual session | What happened | Trace, recording or failing case when available | One focused action | Changed prompt and later result |

Examples of useful feedback include “did not establish who owns the last-seat transition” or “could not explain the metric’s baseline.” A homemade weighted score does not establish an employer’s hiring bar.

## Review the week without creating another dashboard

Compare planned work with actual work in the [weekly schedule](81.21-week-study-schedule.md). Use the [timer](81.11-daily-study-timer.md) for duration and the [review queue](81.10-spaced-repetition-schedule.md) for due dates.

Check whether repairs transferred to changed examples and whether the confirmed interview process has changed. Choose the next priority and an achievable time budget. Totals can describe activity, but do not infer readiness from a fixed hour, problem or story quota.

## Keep assessment logistics separate

Confirm the interview date, format, allowed tools, coding environment and requested accommodations. Test the setup and note unresolved instructions. These are useful completion checks, while correctness and preparation remain supported by the attempt log.

## Summary

Track the task, assistance, observed result and repair. Use later attempts to check retention and transfer, then update the next plan. Keep one learning record rather than repeated daily, weekly and company-specific copies.

## Lane Decision — Is This the Right Lane for You?

Start here when the confirmed process evaluates algorithms, complexity, object design or broader SDE systems work. Add the [abroad full-stack lane](../80-lanes-abroad-full-stack/INDEX.md) for practical implementation, take-homes and relocation research. A vacancy may need both; geography, employer name and years of experience do not establish a universal hiring loop.

Record the language, allowed tools, duration, responsibilities and assessment format from the invitation. Keep unknowns explicit and confirm them before allocating a preparation block.

## Interview Process — Round-by-Round

Treat the stages below as possible formats to confirm, not a fixed sequence or hidden rejection rubric.

| Confirmed stage | Prepare this evidence |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recruiter discussion | Relevant experience, motivation, level expectations and logistical questions |
| Coding exercise | Clarified contract, a derived solution, runnable code, edge-case tests and complexity |
| Implementation/take-home | A bounded working feature, explicit omissions, verification and a useful handoff |
| Design discussion | Requirements, a coherent critical path, capacity assumptions and failure recovery |
| Experience/behavioral discussion | A specific decision you owned, its evidence and what you learned |
| Offer discussion | Written role, level, location and comparable compensation terms |

Use company guides later to formulate questions and research the team. Confirm their current process for the vacancy; no guide can promise its rounds, tools, difficulty or decision.

## Strategy — How to Prep, What to Skip, When to Apply

1. Attempt a representative problem before reading its solution. Record whether the gap is contract, reasoning, implementation, testing or explanation.
2. Study the mechanism in its shared chapter, then reconstruct it without notes.
3. Change an input or constraint and solve again. Include adverse cases such as overflow, cycles, concurrent mutation or an uncertain acknowledgment when relevant.
4. Review a timed mock and choose the next block from its evidence. Keep one feasible schedule rather than several competing calendars.
5. Apply when the role and logistics fit and you can support your claims. Use actual feedback to refine practice; a problem count or number of weeks cannot establish readiness by itself.

For senior roles, go beyond naming a pattern: state the invariant, trace a failure, choose the recovery path and explain a real ownership decision. Use the [senior readiness rubric](../80-lanes-abroad-full-stack/80.29-topic-brushup-roadmap.md) to make those checks observable.

## FAQ

**Q: How many problems or weeks are enough?**

Use fresh, representative attempts and mock feedback to assess readiness. Volume measures activity; it does not establish correctness, transfer or an employer's decision.

**Q: Which language should I choose?**

Confirm the permitted languages, then use one whose data structures, numeric behavior and runtime costs you can explain and implement reliably. A role may also assess its production language.

**Q: Where do compensation and relocation fit?**

Keep them separate from technical readiness. Compare the actual offer through [compensation benchmarking](81.22-compensation-benchmarking.md) and verify work-authorization requirements through the [relocation playbook](../80-lanes-abroad-full-stack/80.06-visa-relocation-playbook.md). Personal terms, statuses and contacts belong in the canonical private workspace.

## When to add to this lane

Add a distinct exercise or dated role observation when it changes the practice decision. Link back to shared mechanisms instead of copying their teaching code. Keep interview observations separate from employer-wide claims and personal application records.

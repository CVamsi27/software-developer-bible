# Organic Reading Flow Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Continue inline from the existing approved explanation-depth design; keep progress and review evidence by document genre.

**Goal:** Make every learning page read as one clear explanation that pulls the reader from a concrete problem through its mechanism to a useful engineering decision, while preserving senior-level accuracy and coverage.

**Architecture:** Use a short, topic-specific opening, the existing practical and textbook definitions, one connected explanation with a worked trace, and only the comparisons or boundaries that change the reader's understanding. Replace generic section sequences with headings that advance each topic. Keep indexes, revision companions, interview drills, case studies and operational records in their own genres.

**Tech Stack:** Markdown, the repository's corpus inventory and verification scripts, Jekyll, existing JavaScript and rendered-page checks.

## Review Standard

For each topic, answer in order: what concrete question brings the reader here; what does the topic mean; what constraint requires its mechanism; what changes in one worked trace; and which boundary changes an engineering decision? Keep only headings that help the reader predict the next step. Introduce formal terms after their plain-language meaning. Explain tables and diagrams in adjacent prose. Preserve useful depth, but move specification and implementation detail after the observable behavior it explains.

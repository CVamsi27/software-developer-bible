# Reading and Interview Quality Design

> **Historical design record:** requirements, example paths and checkboxes describe this dated plan, not current repository status or authorization to rerun it. Later [explanation-depth work](../plans/2026-10-08-explanation-depth.md), [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) and [contributing.md](../../../contributing.md) govern current changes. Preserve stable chapter IDs; TL;DR and inline chapter cheat/revision sections are retired.

The reader currently caps paragraphs twice: at 860px in the theme include and at 76 characters in the reading stylesheet. At desktop widths this leaves unused article space. Use one article width, with responsive gutters; paragraphs, lists and callouts fill that area. Keep wide tables and code independently scrollable.

Preserve each existing practical definition. Add a concise, original textbook-style definition that states the formal mechanism and its boundary. These are explanatory paraphrases, not quotations from an unnamed textbook. Do not add definitions to navigation pages, revision drills or dated application records merely to satisfy a template.

Review the complete Markdown inventory with existing grammar, structure, depth, freshness and duplication scanners. Read chapter introductions and flagged passages for topic-specific edits. Improve concrete mechanisms, failure cases and decision criteria; keep revision and interview practice in their existing companions. Preserve numbered IDs, authored questions and personal-data boundaries.

Prioritize senior TypeScript/React/Node/PostgreSQL roles, matching the current resume. Readiness means explaining a mechanism, implementing it, diagnosing failure and defending a trade-off under follow-up questions. Add an observable practice rubric to the existing study route, rather than another competing curriculum.

Theme verification covers system and explicit preferences, persistence, body/code/table/callout contrast, long content, desktop/mobile widths and keyboard access. Resume changes must follow the existing claim crosswalk. Keep experience titles and dates, supported scope and measured outcomes; studying a technology does not establish professional experience.

A wholesale rewrite would put working examples and historical records at risk. A grammar-only sweep would leave missing definitions and interview decision depth untouched. The selected approach is a full inventory with targeted editorial changes, full textbook-definition coverage for concept chapters, and browser-verified reader repairs.

Release evidence consists of the complete repository verification harness, regenerated counts/read times/search/API catalog, browser assertions and screenshots, resume tests and visual PDF inspection. Local validation does not establish a deployed change; this request does not require publication.

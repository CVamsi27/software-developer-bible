> **Historical design record:** requirements, example paths, and checkboxes describe this dated plan, not current repository status or authorization to rerun it. Later [explanation-depth work](../plans/2026-10-08-explanation-depth.md), [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md), and [contributing.md](../../../contributing.md) govern current changes. Preserve stable chapter IDs; `TL;DR` and inline chapter cheat and revision sections are retired.

The reader currently caps paragraphs twice: at 860 pixels in the theme include and at 76 characters in the reading stylesheet. At desktop widths, this leaves unused article space. Use one article width with responsive gutters so paragraphs, lists, and callouts fill that area while keeping wide tables and code independently scrollable.

Preserve each existing practical definition. Add a concise, original textbook-style definition that states the formal mechanism and its boundary — explanatory paraphrases rather than quotations from an unnamed textbook. Do not add definitions to navigation pages, revision drills, or dated application records merely to satisfy a template.

Review the complete Markdown inventory with existing grammar, structure, depth, freshness, and duplication scanners. Read chapter introductions and flagged passages for topic-specific edits to improve concrete mechanisms, failure cases, and decision criteria while keeping revision and interview practice in their existing companions. Preserve numbered IDs, authored questions, and personal-data boundaries.

Prioritize senior TypeScript, React, Node, and PostgreSQL roles, matching the current resume. Readiness means explaining a mechanism, implementing it, diagnosing failures, and defending trade-offs under follow-up questions; add an observable practice rubric to the existing study route rather than a competing curriculum.

Theme verification covers system and explicit preferences, persistence, body, code, table, and callout contrast, long content, desktop and mobile widths, and keyboard access. Resume changes follow the existing claim crosswalk: keep experience titles and dates, supported scope, and measured outcomes, since studying a technology does not establish professional experience.

A wholesale rewrite would put working examples and historical records at risk, while a grammar-only sweep would leave missing definitions and interview decision depth untouched. The selected approach is a full inventory with targeted editorial changes, textbook-definition coverage for concept chapters, and browser-verified reader repairs.

Release evidence consists of the complete repository verification harness, regenerated counts, read times, search, and API catalog, browser assertions and screenshots, resume tests, and visual PDF inspection. Local validation does not establish a deployed change; this request does not require publication.

## How to pick a lane

### Pick **Indian SDE / FAANG** if:
- You're targeting Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta, Apple, or in-country India product companies
- The phone screen has LeetCode medium-to-hard
- You have 8-12 weeks at 4-6 hrs/day
- Comp target: USD 150-400K+ (US FAANG) or INR 30-120 L (India in-country)

### Pick **Abroad Full-Stack** if:
- You're targeting EU mid-stage startups (n8n, SumUp, Scalable, Tanso, LiveEO, Snke, MVST)
- The interview is take-home heavy
- You have 4-6 weeks at 2-3 hrs/day
- Comp target: EUR 75-100K base + 0.05-0.5% equity + visa sponsorship (canonical ranges: [80.05](../80-lanes-abroad-full-stack/80.05-comp-eu-benchmarking.md))
- You have a family (NL 30% ruling, EU Blue Card, schools) — geography matters

### Use **both lanes** if:
- You're targeting Booking.com, Spotify, Stripe, or remote-first US companies
- You're in between on the LeetCode/take-home spectrum
- You want the FAANG-DSA signal **and** the EU-specific family/visa overlay

## Lanes are terminal

Lanes are **terminal** — they reference the stacks, never the other way around. The 7 study stacks (numbered: `10-frontend` → `20-backend` → `30-architecture` → `40-platform` → `50-quality` → `60-realtime` → `70-interview-toolkit`) contain no lane-specific content; the [00-strategy](../00-strategy/INDEX.md) folder holds the cross-lane material (resume, STAR, communication) and personalization goes in the lane.

**Rule of thumb:** if you're modifying code in the shared folders (`00-strategy` or any of the 7 stacks), ask "does this apply to **both** lanes?" If yes, edit the stack. If no, edit the lane.

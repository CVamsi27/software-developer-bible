## Choose the assessed work

Confirm the employer's actual interview format before committing weeks of study — a company name or country alone never guarantees a fixed loop, preparation length, salary band, or visa policy.

| What your target loop tests | Recommended lane | Primary practice |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Practical full-stack building, take-homes, EU/remote startup loops, and relocation | [Abroad Full-Stack Lane](../80-lanes-abroad-full-stack/INDEX.md) | Build a scoped feature with tests, defend architecture choices, and research live roles |
| Timed algorithms, data structures, complexity analysis, and Big Tech / Indian SDE loops | [Indian SDE / FAANG Lane (repository)](https://github.com/CVamsi27/software-developer-bible/blob/main/81-lanes-indian-sde-faang/INDEX.md) | Solve unseen coding problems cleanly, trace edge cases, and explain system trade-offs |

Many senior roles blend both formats (for example, one DSA screen plus a practical React/Node pairing round and a system design session). Use the [lane decision guide](../80-lanes-abroad-full-stack/INDEX.md#lane-decision--is-this-the-right-lane-for-you) to combine them cleanly.

In the hosted web reader, the Indian SDE / FAANG lane stays in the repository; use [Coding Patterns](../70-interview-toolkit/70.2-coding-patterns/INDEX.md), [System Design](../30-architecture/30.2-system-design/INDEX.md), and the core language stacks for all shared technical lessons.

## Move from reading to interview readiness

Reading alone creates false confidence. Turn every study block into a five-step loop:

1. **Test first:** Try a representative problem or design prompt to expose your real gaps.
2. **Study the mechanism:** Read the canonical chapter in the shared stack and trace its examples.
3. **Recall cold:** Close the chapter and answer its category `REVISION.md` questions without notes.
4. **Vary a constraint:** Re-run the exercise with a harder constraint (scale, latency, failure mode, or time cap).
5. **Review and pick next:** Use the [senior readiness rubric](../80-lanes-abroad-full-stack/80.29-topic-brushup-roadmap.md) to judge implementation quality, failure analysis, and trade-off defense before moving on.

## Keep technical lessons and personal records cleanly separated

The [7 study stacks](../INDEX.md) are the single canonical home for technical concepts. Lanes organize role-specific practice and link into those stacks rather than duplicating explanations. Keep personal CVs, recruiter contacts, application statuses, and family relocation plans strictly in your private workspace, never in this public repository.

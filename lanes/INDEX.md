## Choose the assessed work

| Need | Lane | Practice |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Practical full-stack implementation, take-homes and role research | [Abroad full-stack](../80-lanes-abroad-full-stack/INDEX.md) | Build a bounded feature, test it and defend its design |
| Algorithms, complexity and broader SDE systems work | [SDE / FAANG (repository)](https://github.com/CVamsi27/software-developer-bible/blob/main/81-lanes-indian-sde-faang/INDEX.md) | Derive fresh solutions, trace edge cases and explain trade-offs |

A role may require both. Use the [lane decision](../80-lanes-abroad-full-stack/INDEX.md#lane-decision--is-this-the-right-lane-for-you) to connect the paths. Country and employer labels do not establish fixed rounds, preparation time, compensation or sponsorship.

The SDE/FAANG lane guides are repository references. In this reader, use [coding patterns](../70-interview-toolkit/70.2-coding-patterns/INDEX.md), [system design](../30-architecture/30.2-system-design/INDEX.md), and the language chapters for the shared learning material.

## Move from learning to practice

1. Diagnose a gap through a representative attempt.
2. Follow the shared stack's learning stages and example.
3. Answer its revision companion without notes.
4. Practice the confirmed interview format with a changed constraint.
5. Review the evidence and choose the next block.

Use the [senior readiness rubric](../80-lanes-abroad-full-stack/80.29-topic-brushup-roadmap.md) to assess implementation, failure analysis, judgment and ownership.

## Keep the shared lessons canonical

The [study stacks](../INDEX.md) own technical explanations. Lanes organize role-specific practice and link to those lessons. Keep personal CVs, contacts, application status and relocation records in the canonical private workspace.
